# Research library

A research packet records what a research session found, what it could check, and which questions remain open. A reviewed J1 scientific claim describes a specific result in a defined population, comparison, outcome and time horizon. A programming policy describes a choice the coach could make. Accepting a claim does not establish when a planner should act on it.

Recovery and autoregulation came forward to inform joint strength and running decisions. J3 scientific curation and practical method development continue independently. ### Scientific review blockers

- Map overlapping primary studies in Schumann, Petré and Huiberts and across the running reviews. Verify interaction estimates for timing, training status, sex and modality. A significant result in one subgroup and an uncertain result in another cannot establish a difference between them.
- Keep maximal strength, explosive strength, biopsy-measured fiber hypertrophy and whole-muscle hypertrophy distinct. Verify the contributing populations, muscle groups, measures and intervals for each contrast.
- Separate running economy at a specified speed or fatigue state, time to exhaustion and race or time-trial performance. Determine habitual lifting exposure before inferring an incremental benefit for runners who already strength train.
- Check the duration of each contributing comparison against its draft scope. For example, C11 reports 6–14 weeks for the high-load running-economy comparison, while `high-load-running-economy@1` uses the overall 6–24-week horizon. Check the Petré timing strata and Llanos-Lagos performance contrasts separately. Leave the proposed records intact for adjudication.
- Obtain the primary reports for abstract-based results, including Gomes, Støren, Trowell and Zanini. Verify direct between-group contrasts, intervals, realized training and baseline experience. Confirm versions of record, correction notices, cohort overlap and reuse rights. ### Nutrition scientific review blockers

- Distinguish energy balance from energy availability (EA). Verify the exposure and comparator in low-EA studies. Stable weight cannot establish adequate EA. Sports medicine and dietetics reviewers must determine referral boundaries and assess corrected REDs guidance. Automated REDs diagnosis, clinical risk scoring and a universal EA threshold are outside scope.
- Check the measured endpoint and time horizon. Acute muscle protein synthesis does not establish chronic hypertrophy or strength gains. Glycogen restoration does not establish subsequent performance. Distinguish direct concurrent-training comparisons from transfer across disciplines or to athletes experienced in both.
- Extract Hartono’s protein study counts separately from pooled effects. Check the population, model and uncertainty of Morton’s breakpoint before interpreting it. A population estimate cannot establish an individual requirement.
- Check units, populations and conditions for consensus reference ranges. Review uncertainty in wearable expenditure and food logs, including which reference method and device version were used. A study-average error does not calibrate an individual record. Personalized total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) estimation remains a Packet 007 research question.
- Verify versions of record, corrections, reuse rights and overlapping cohorts, including reused Caldwell performance data and primary studies shared by reviews. ### What intake and weight can identify

| Quantity | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| True physiological total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) | Energy expended over a specified day, or its mean over a specified interval. The energy-balance identity relates metabolizable intake and expenditure to the change in **stored chemical energy**. |
| Conditional expenditure estimate | An estimate under stated assumptions about reporting bias, tissue change or device calibration. Report how the estimate varies across defensible assumptions. |
| Operational logged maintenance intake | Mean reported daily intake associated with a practically stable observed mass trajectory under a sufficiently stable logging regime and representative activity. A change in recording habits or training can make the historical reference stale. |
| Approved reported-calorie target | The athlete’s agreed intended intake in reported calories. Approval records a decision. Actual intake and adherence require their own observations. |
| Observed response | Changes in comparable scale measurements and performance. Fluid shifts and changes in tissue composition allow the same mass trajectory to accompany different changes in stored energy. Stable mass alone cannot establish physiological adequacy. |

Under the packet’s additive observation model, increasing true intake, true expenditure and underreporting bias by the same feasible offset, while decreasing wearable bias by that offset, leaves logged intake, wearable readings and energy balance identical. Even noiseless free-living observations cannot distinguish those explanations. Bayesian regularization can favor one explanation while the likelihood remains identical. Independently justified bounds on bias and storage change give a conditional range of compatible expenditures. The archive distinguishes that range from sampling uncertainty and preserves the derivation, tissue-composition counterexample and sensitivity analysis.
