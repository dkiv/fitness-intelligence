## 1. Executive summary

**Central decision:** given the current approved hybrid block and incomplete observations, choose a defensible action (continue, progress, hold, modify, redistribute, reduce, ask, defer or revise), not a scalar estimate of whether the athlete is recovered. Readiness is a hypothesis about a particular task on a particular day. Local, general, perceptual and autonomic responses need not agree. Training can remain productive despite residual fatigue.

- The most immediately useful inputs are actual exposure, comparable performance, task purpose, restrictions and targeted feedback. Treat this priority as candidate methodology, not a demonstrated ranking of predictor utility.
- RIR estimates contain error. A controlled study in trained men and a pooled analysis support investigating RPE-based load selection as an alternative to fixed percentages; superiority is uncertain. Volume autoregulation is a separate question. No exact load or set adjustment follows from reporting a given RPE.
- Nuuttila's 2022 randomized running intervention is direct evidence for a *bundled* adaptive approach, not evidence that HRV alone is necessary. It adapted running duration and interval frequency; the participants did very little other training. It cannot establish the incremental value of any signal or efficacy in hybrid athletes.
- HRV-guided endurance reviews report uncertain performance benefits. HRV reflects cardiac autonomic modulation under a particular protocol, not whole-body readiness. High or low values can have multiple interpretations; unverified HRV units prohibit quantitative use.
- Acute sleep loss can impair exercise performance. Sleep extension and naps warrant consideration, but sleep-based automatic cancellation rules are unvalidated. Consumer sleep staging is not a direct measure of restoration.
- Resistance exercise near failure can increase subsequent neuromuscular fatigue compared with distributing the same repetitions across more sets. Acute running impairment after lifting depends on task and protocol; this does not establish a fixed recovery interval or chronic interference.
- Fitness–fatigue models may fit retrospective performance while leaving parameters poorly identified. Start with discipline-specific descriptive exposures and comparable performance; reserve modeling and incremental-value evaluation for Packet 009.

**Minimum useful J3 foundation:** bounded strength adjustments, adherence and performance review, targeted feedback, task-specific scheduling, safe hold/reduction and explicit approval. HRV, sleep stages, velocity sensors, composite scores and latent-state estimation are optional or deferred. Independent source, transfer, method and numerical parameter review remains mandatory. Scientific readiness, observation readiness and operational authorization are independent.

Acute fatigue is a transient loss of capacity or altered subjective effort after work; residual fatigue persists into later tasks; accumulated fatigue is a description of repeated burden and response, not a uniquely observable state. Neuromuscular fatigue may be tested through standardized force/velocity or stimulation protocols; metabolic fatigue through task-specific physiological measures; mechanical tolerance through actual tissue-relevant exposure and symptoms, not HR alone. Psychological fatigue and stress are partly directly accessible via self-report, but not direct measures of muscle damage or energy status. Recovery is restoration relative to a defined function and timeframe; readiness also depends on skill, motivation, fueling, environment and task. Normal soreness, exertion and occasional poor performance do not establish harm. Persistent unexplained deterioration, significant pain, illness symptoms, suspected REDs or sleep disorder require professional assessment rather than algorithmic diagnosis. Chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness or other urgent symptoms warrant stopping activity and urgent medical care. No wearable output confers clearance.

Targeted questions (e.g., which task feels impaired, new pain versus normal soreness, unusual stress, whether reps were intentionally stopped, recent fueling) are a low-burden candidate alternative to fixed daily batteries. No retrieved trial establishes conversational equivalence to validated questionnaires or incremental benefit of every questionnaire item. Record scale anchors and time of report; distinguish missing responses, habituation, social desirability, recall and changing interpretation. Within-athlete patterns with stable questions are preferable to comparing scores across athletes as if calibrated.

APRE adjusts load from performance on specified sets; RPE/RIR prescriptions select load to meet an effort target; velocity-based schemes use measured speed (load–velocity profiles or within-set speed loss); flexible daily undulation selects a planned session variant; double progression uses rep completion and effort. These are different interventions. Packet 006 `flexible-dup-powerlifting-total@1` and Packet 001 `load-vs-repetition-progression@1` remain relevant, not evidence of every variation. For exercise selection/schedule/block-level changes, direct efficacy remains a gap. An RPE 8 report on an anchored lifting scale is an estimate of roughly two remaining repetitions, not a measured 2 RIR. Distinguish prescription, reported effort, actual reps, observed technical/task failure, and physiological effort. Higher reps, early set estimates, unfamiliar exercises, changed rest or accumulated fatigue can affect estimates; individual practice may help but no reviewed calibration schedule. Session RPE (overall exertion) is not interchangeable with set RPE or soreness rating. Do not require failure tests for calibration or use a missed rep as permission to test failure again. Velocity needs same movement, load, intent, device and technique; no universal conversion to RIR.

Sleep timing, regularity, quality and opportunity should be discussed separately from duration and staging. Naps or extra opportunity may be feasible but athlete insomnia or persistent daytime sleepiness warrants qualified assessment. Acute deprivation literature does not identify a sleep threshold for easy runs, heavy lifts or cancellations. No retrieved head-to-head trial establishes sleep-stage-driven training adjustment. For HRV, RMSSD = root mean square of successive normal interbeat interval differences; lnRMSSD is a transformed quantity; nocturnal average, morning supine/standing spot, seven-day summary and frequency-domain power are distinct. HRV may reflect autonomic modulation, posture, respiration, signal quality, alcohol, infection, travel, stress, nutrition and training. Neither high nor low HRV has a unique physiological interpretation. Resting HR depends on method and context too. Don't mix methods, devices, summary windows or unknown units. A single night is not a trend; an overlapping rolling average is not an independent daily observation. No quantified response to every listed stressor was extracted; these are plausible confounders requiring targeted review, not coefficients.

Cross-modal review extends Packet 003, not a claim that lifting necessarily worsens long-term running. Failure sets, eccentric novelty, long runs, races, threshold work and intermittent cutting/jumping sports impose different local mechanical and metabolic burdens. Quantitative recovery curves for all of these, particularly in dual-trained athletes, remain missing. Similar heart rate or calories do not establish equivalent local load. Protect the task that matters; avoid automatically reducing upper-body work for lower-limb soreness, or requiring global rest for isolated autonomic deviations.

Nutrition and weight historical coverage not established. No requirements for a specific provider.

### 2.7 Incremental decision value and modeling (RE-10/11)

Evidence ladder: (i) agreement with reference, (ii) repeatability, (iii) response to known exposure, (iv) temporal prediction beyond baseline, (v) better decisions/outcomes when used. F02 and F03 mostly iii; F27–28 i; F22 exploratory association not validated iv; F15–21 v for entire intervention but not its components. No retrieved trial compares all observation tiers A–F or validates a composite readiness score for experienced hybrid athletes. The correct result is an evidence gap, not proof that HRV or questionnaires are useless.

| Candidate observation set | Potential decisions | Evidence gap / evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| A: goal/history + fixed program | coherent prescription | comparator, not automatically poor coaching |
| B: A + actual completion and comparable performance | hold/progress/defer based on actual exposure | evaluate vs A; minimal adaptive baseline |
| C: B + subjective recovery/soreness | targeted local/general changes, contextual safety | evaluate incremental value vs B, response burden; not established by F02 alone |
| D: C + sleep duration/resting HR | investigate broader context | test incremental prediction, false alarms vs C |
| E: D + HRV | optional corroboration or evaluated adaptive strategy | F16/F19 not E vs D; unverified units block quantitative MVP use |
| F: E + richer physiological/performance data | targeted measurement where it could change action | calibration, test burden and prospective value vs E |

Fitness–fatigue impulse response models represent performance as baseline + decayed positive and negative responses to training inputs. Sparse correlated exposure and outcomes can identify fitted predictions without identifying underlying states. Unknown baseline, time constants, noise, changing training, competing modalities and unmeasured nutrition create nonidentifiability; fitting more parameters increases risk of overfitting. Use separate rolling/exponentially weighted *descriptive* exposure summaries with clearly documented window and missingness, not physiological units, injury thresholds or a shared arbitrary workload scalar. Compare against last observation, simple trends and fixed/feedback policies using time-ordered held-out data, calibration, error intervals and prospective evaluation. No estimator, numeric score or reinforcement-learning implementation here; P009 owns formal models and causal study design.

## 3. Candidate coaching policies

### 3.1 Shared contract

These are **candidate research methods**, not executable rules. All policies below are pending independent human review, with no operational authorization. Earlier policies retain their exact revisions; extensions are conceptual and do not supersede them. The scientific readiness assessments are provisional; data readiness is conditional on canonical observation availability, eligibility and coverage, not particular providers. Distinguish **feasible** (required observations can be supplied), **conditionally feasible** (specific coverage, feedback or comparison conditions must be satisfied), **qualitative only** (contextual guidance, not quantitative individualized inference), and **deferred** (minimum observations/validation absent). A feasible policy may still lack scientific review. A safety restriction takes precedence over an ordinary training preference; inability to verify recovery does not itself mean the athlete must rest. Where ordinary training is still appropriate, retain reviewed plan bounds, current objectives, calendar, actual-vs-prescribed distinctions, provenance and approval requirements from P006. Do not make RPE, HRV, sleep or macro tracking mandatory simply because they can improve interpretation.

For all policies, retain exact prescribed vs actual work, scope and time of observations, measurement provenance and eligibility, alternatives considered, uncertainty, restrictions, and policy/evidence revision. A proposal changes nothing until accepted. In-session execution choices inside an already authorized range do not rewrite the prescription; out-of-range or future prescription changes require explicit athlete approval. A changed restriction invalidates a pending proposal and requires revalidation; unsafe exercise should stop regardless of approval workflow. Clinical red flags, significant new pain, acute illness or suspected REDs require appropriate qualified assessment rather than diagnostic inference. The policy never overrides a medical restriction. All examples use provisional contextual bounds, no universal thresholds.

### RE-P01 revision 0.1 — Recovery observation eligibility (extends EN-P10)

- **Decision / construct:** admit an observation to a particular comparison, treat it as qualitative context, or exclude it; measurement error, coverage and task relevance.
- **Science:** F04, F27–28, F31; reference agreement does not guarantee within-athlete change validity. Eligibility is engineering judgment, not treatment efficacy.
- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal validated repeated measurements with protocol and artifact flags; minimum canonical meaning, timestamp, actual vs planned, unit/scale where quantitative, method and coverage sufficient for intended comparison; optional equipment, terrain, timing, device changes and athlete clarification.
- **Prescription / adherence / effectiveness:** prescription needs goals, constraints and eligible relevant history, not every recovery measure. Adherence needs evidence of actual work or explicit partial/miss; effectiveness needs comparable outcome series, context and uncertainty. Mark missing separately from zero/normal.
- **Readiness:** science preliminary for proxy-specific findings; data **feasible** for structural eligibility, **conditional** for comparable performance, **deferred** for quantitative unknown-unit HRV or unverified trace-dependent quantities.
- **Logic / assumptions:** normalize only verified equivalent units/definitions, preserve source identity, flag changes in method as discontinuities, exclude stale or incomparable observations from quantitative decisions. Prefer a like-for-like task over a derived global score. Never assume two providers with the same label have equivalent protocols.
- **Missing / fallback / prohibited:** retain otherwise eligible inputs; ask a targeted question if eligibility changes the action; no imputation of normal recovery or completed intervals from planned steps. No interpolating a new baseline across devices without validation.
- **Extensions / status:** high-resolution traces, quality flags, protocol-specific reliability calibration, explicit canonical mapping tests. Pending review; no authorization.

### RE-P02 revision 0.1 — Targeted subjective feedback

- **Decision / construct:** whether a short question could change a decision about task tolerance, local soreness, general fatigue, perceived stress, motivation, pain or fueling.
- **Science:** F01–03; F19 includes subjective observations in a bundle. Questionnaire responsiveness does not demonstrate optimal daily reporting or incremental efficacy. Conversational approach is methodology.
- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal repeated validated anchored scales plus relevant context; minimum meaningful question and athlete-attributed dated report when needed; optional location, severity/function, change from usual, sleep, stress, enjoyment, recent food, reasons for stopping.
- **Prescription / adherence / effectiveness:** prescription may proceed on eligible history absent feedback unless decision specifically depends on symptoms or effort. Adherence requires actual completion/deviation, not motivation alone. Effectiveness uses comparable outcomes and repeated feedback, not a single favorable response.
- **Readiness:** science preliminary for responsiveness, incremental value unresolved; **conditionally feasible** with voluntary response; qualitative when isolated.
- **Logic / assumptions:** ask only questions whose answers could change branch; distinguish local vs general, ordinary soreness vs pain/restriction, exertion vs inability to perform; treat volunteered restrictions as constraints. Use consistent anchors, not population thresholds.
- **Missing / fallback / prohibited:** no response means unknown; proceed with independent defensible conservative plan or defer affected increase. Do not diagnose muscle damage, recovery capacity or overreaching from a score. If concerning symptoms, prioritize appropriate care.
- **Extensions / status:** opt-in validated questionnaires, evaluate burden and calibration against simpler methods; pending, no authorization.

### RE-P03 revision 0.1 — RPE/RIR-aware strength prescription (extends RT-P01)

- **Decision / construct:** set and adjust load/repetitions within approved exercise-specific effort and execution bounds; proximity to task failure, task performance, specificity.
- **Science:** F04–07, F09, P001 load-vs-rep claim; RPE is imperfect estimate, pooled superiority uncertain. Double progression, contextual increment and effort range are programming choices.
- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal matched repeated sets with calibrated effort and measured load/velocity; minimum exercise identity/variant, actual load/reps, prior comparable exposure, goal, constraints, approved range, optional actual RPE when available; enhanced rest time, technique, rep stop reason, fatigue and relevant run exposure. For an RPE-targeted *quantitative change* require anchored actual effort or independently sufficient load-rep evidence under a reviewed alternative rule.
- **Prescription / adherence / effectiveness:** generate from recent comparable sets and bounds; adherence from actual sets/reps/load and whether stopped/missed, effort optional for dose but needed to evaluate effort compliance; effectiveness from comparable later performance, effort/context, not estimated 1RM alone.
- **Readiness:** narrow efficacy support preliminary, no verified hybrid-specific optimal rule; **conditionally feasible**, including RPE-free reviewed load-rep branch.
- **Logic / assumptions:** if comparable target completed within reviewed effort allowance and progression advances objective, consider rep or load increment inside bounds. If target felt easier but progress is already adequate, hold permissible. Unexpectedly high effort or missed reps: hold increase, clarify rest/technique/local burden, consider lower load or fewer reps within authorized range or propose change. Repeated rising effort across sets may prompt review of rest/dose, not automatic additions. If RPE missing, use comparable actual work only; do not reconstruct RIR.
- **Missing / fallback / prohibited:** repeat feasible prior dose or ask targeted feedback; no exact remaining repetitions, arbitrary RPE-to-kg formula, required failure test or escalation from one easy set.
- **Extensions / status:** standardized warmup, exercise-specific error characterization, velocity if validated; pending, no authorization.

### RE-P04 revision 0.1 — Autoregulated sets and weekly strength volume (extends RT-P02/CT-P05)

- **Decision / construct:** retain, redistribute, add or reduce working sets; distinguish in-session termination, reps per set and weekly exposure. Adaptation versus recoverable burden.
- **Science:** F08, F10–13, Packet 002 fixed escalation and RPE-stop study (Helms 2018, DOI 10.1519/JSC.0000000000002032: 12 powerlifters, three weeks, volume differed with back-off strategy, not long-term superiority). No validated feedback-based weekly strength-volume algorithm.
- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal actual muscle/exercise-specific dose, performance, effort, tolerance and both disciplines; minimum eligible completed working sets by exercise, recent habitual exposure, objectives, time, approved bounds, observed performance or credible tolerance report; optional RIR, rest, soreness, running intensity, fueling.
- **Prescription / adherence / effectiveness:** planned sets and roles from baseline; actual completed working sets and reps for adherence, not all logged sets automatically; subsequent lift performance and relevant secondary running outcome, burden and time for effectiveness; hypertrophy requires independent suitable assessment.
- **Readiness:** low for adaptive efficacy; **conditional** on dose/comparison coverage; velocity-threshold method **deferred** without validated velocity.
- **Logic / assumptions:** productive dose is a valid endpoint. Compare actual to planned before changing dose. If sufficient evidence suggests insufficient stimulus and tolerance allows, consider reviewed addition; if burden high or local tolerance poor, hold or reduce/redistribute specific work. Protect maintenance commitments. Terminate unachievable or unsafe sets; do not retroactively mark them complete. Changes in reps, sets and load jointly affect realized exposure; do not claim isolated causal benefit.
- **Missing / fallback / prohibited:** hold pending feedback or review, no invented universal increment, fatigue budget or conversion of velocity loss to RIR. No mandatory increases when all work done.
- **Extensions / status:** richer set performance and effort, prospective comparison against fixed volume; pending, no authorization.

### RE-P05 revision 0.1 — Recovery-aware running prescription and calendar (extends EN-P02/04/09, CT-P02)

- **Decision / construct:** adjust running intensity, duration or timing within purpose; local mechanical tolerance, task readiness, endurance adaptation.
- **Science:** F15–21 bundled intervention; F29 acute cross-modal evidence; P003 timing, P005 progression, P006 priority calendar. No direct validated hybrid algorithm or universal interval.
- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal actual interval outcomes, comparable pace/HR/effort, actual concurrent exposures, fatigue and fueling; minimum approved run purpose/dose, actual recent training, schedule/constraints and a meaningful indication of tolerance/performance if changing for recovery; optional verified HRV, RHR, sleep, detailed intervals, environment.
- **Prescription / adherence / effectiveness:** generate a coherent run from current goals and history even without HRV; adherence from completed activity and actual intensity evidence or report (instructions alone insufficient); effectiveness from repeated matched sessions or outcome assessments plus strength retention.
- **Readiness:** promising endurance-only evidence; hybrid transfer low; **conditional** for scheduling and dose proposal, **qualitative only** if no comparable performance, **deferred** for exact replication of study algorithm without protocol inputs.
- **Logic / assumptions:** first preserve priority task; consider moving demanding work, substituting a reviewed easy session, reducing duration or maintaining. Repeated poor quality after specific loading is a hypothesis to test. Before adding intensity investigate adherence, training stimulus and nutritional/schedule feasibility. No automatic cancellation of all running from a recovery metric.
- **Missing / fallback / prohibited:** retain plan or use reviewed qualitative options; ask about achieved effort, local soreness, weather and fueling if relevant; do not infer precise thresholds, achieved intervals or chronic interference from aggregate activity summary.
- **Extensions / status:** paired time-series, standardized running measures and validated optional recovery inputs; pending, no authorization.

### RE-P06 revision 0.1 — HRV and resting HR contextual interpretation

- **Decision / construct:** whether eligible autonomic observations warrant clarification or support a task-specific proposal; cardiac autonomic regulation and measurement context, not global readiness.

### RE-P07 revision 0.1 — Sleep-aware training context

- **Decision / construct:** whether sleep opportunity, quality or disruption changes the feasibility of a specific session; acute performance and longer-term recovery context.

- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal validated sleep timing/duration and subjective quality over comparable periods; minimum dated report or eligible duration summary plus intended session and context; optional regularity, shift/travel, symptoms, naps. No stage tracking required.
- **Prescription / adherence / effectiveness:** generate ordinary prescriptions without sleep data; if modifying for sleep require relevant observation and task context; actual exposure for adherence, subsequent task performance and tolerance for effectiveness.

### RE-P08 revision 0.1 — Performance-based recovery review (extends EN-P07, RT-P01, HP-P09)

- **Decision / construct:** assess whether actual task performance suggests review of dose, effort or scheduling; distinguish transient suppression, adaptation and error.

- **Ideal / minimum / optional:** ideal repeated standardized performance, actual exposure, matched conditions and uncertainty; minimum comparable actual outcome with date, exercise/run protocol, relevant exposure and known confounders; optional effort, test repeatability, bar velocity, environment, sleep and fueling.

Repeated deterioration prompts competing explanations and local or general response. Do not chase one test result or force maximal assessments.

### RE-P09 revision 0.1 — Local/general fatigue and cross-discipline coordination (extends CT-P02/05, HP-P07)

- **Decision / construct:** local reduction, session redistribution, general recovery or unchanged training; muscle-specific burden, mechanical tolerance, perceived systemic strain and conflicting objectives.

## 4. Periodization methodology

### 4.1 Recovery-aware hierarchical workflow

Recovery decisions inherit P006 long-term strategy, block outcome commitments, phase intent, calendar and permitted execution ranges. A normal execution choice is not a new prescription; a changed future session, week or block is a proposal. The aim is a feasible next action and explicit uncertainty, not an estimate of how much recovery capacity remains. Keep actual histories by discipline, movement and task; don't collapse mechanical and metabolic exposure into an unvalidated total.

| Step | Candidate procedure | Output / guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Load approved block and current plan head, primary/secondary commitments and protected sessions | Explicit objective, phase and allowed bounds; no inferred priorities |
| 2 | Review completed lifting, running and other sport | Separate actual, partial, missed, unknown and planned exposures, timestamps and relevant body region |
| 3 | Compare actual exposure to intended | Identify departures, increased burden, missing work, schedule issues; do not equate log absence with skipped session |
| 4 | Apply RE-P01 eligibility | Exclude ineligible HRV, ambiguous units, mismatched exercises, changed terrain, etc. from quantitative comparison |
| 5 | Evaluate actual task performance and tolerance | Comparable outcomes, direction, uncertainty, not a latent state label |
| 6 | Identify local/general concerns | Athlete report and task-specific pattern; allow unknown or mixed; no diagnosis |
| 7 | Consider subjective and eligible recovery observations | Task relevance, protocol, repeated vs isolated observations; no weighted score |
| 8 | Enumerate plausible explanations | Exposure/adherence, stimulus, scheduling, sleep, illness, stress, fueling, environment, measurement error |
| 9 | Screen safety, restrictions and feasibility | Stop/defer unsafe work and recommend appropriate assessment; ordinary fatigue is not automatically a safety restriction |
| 10 | Generate feasible candidate actions | Continue/hold; progress bounded load/reps/sets or run duration/intensity; redistribute; local/general reduction; fueling clarification; recovery period; defer or revision |
| 11 | Check candidates against block constraints | Time, phase, assessment protection, specific maintenance commitments, approved progression bounds |
| 12 | Choose defensible proposal or request missing information | Prefer less disruptive option when equivalent for objectives; explain uncertainty; no unique optimal action asserted |
| 13 | Explicitly protect secondary objectives | Review retention with actual comparable outcomes; if conflict cannot be solved within bounds propose revised commitments |
| 14 | Obtain athlete decision | Read-only guidance vs execution choices vs new prescription/week/block; revalidate current head and restrictions at approval; decline leaves approved plan intact |
| 15 | Evaluate subsequent response | Record actual outcomes, adherence, uncertainty, context; no causal attribution from a before/after comparison |

**Branch logic (illustrative, nonoperational):** first safety/restrictions, then data eligibility, then current task and objective, then comparison and uncertainty. If no evidence of relevant deterioration and plan productive, continue/hold is permissible. If credible improvement plus objective needs additional stimulus and feasibility supports it, propose bounded progression. If relevant local problem with unaffected tasks, preserve unaffected work; choose dose reduction, alternative movement, easier run or rescheduling only if consistent with restrictions. If generalized decline, hold escalation, investigate causes and consider coordinated reduction/assessment. If uncertainty prevents meaningful choice, targeted question or defer. No rule that every low HRV, high RPE or poor night's sleep triggers reduction; no rule that all conflicting observations can be ignored if one favorable value exists.

### 4.2 Conflicting observations

| Pattern | Relevant hypotheses and checks | Defensible candidate action; what not to infer |
|---|---|---|
| Low HRV, improving task performance | Units/method, overnight change, recent load and stress, symptoms | If eligible and no other concern, continue/hold or ask; low HRV not proof of maladaptation |
| Normal HRV, declining comparable performance | Task exposure, effort, adherence, sleep/fueling, illness, measurement | Review task, hold escalation, adjust if warranted; HRV not clearance |
| High subjective fatigue, acceptable performance | General stress vs local strain, sustainability, impending demand | Discuss preference/burden, preserve or modestly reduce relevant nonpriority work; performance doesn't negate experience |
| Low fatigue report, deteriorating performance | Changed effort, reporting anchors, inadequate exposure, protocol | Review and clarify; don't infer denial or force extra training |
| Poor sleep, easy session | Tolerability, symptoms, safety, intended purpose | Continue if appropriate or allow reviewed easier alternative; no sleep-hours cutoff |
| Good sleep, persistent local soreness | Prior eccentric/loading novelty, pain/restriction, affected task | Retain unaffected work or propose local change; no inference of full recovery |
| High lift RPE after hard run / slower threshold after lower-body work | Temporal ordering, usual dose, matched task, effort and fueling | Move or reduce relevant stress as experiment; no certainty of interference |
| Stable weight/apparently adequate calories with persistent fatigue | Logging error, EA, carbohydrate timing, sleep/stress/illness | Ask and review both disciplines; qualify nutrition advice or refer; weight stability not adequate EA |
| Multiple concerning signs with possible illness | Symptoms, restrictions, persistence, clinical assessment | Defer demanding training and recommend appropriate assessment; no automated diagnosis |

| Quantity / source | Classification and observed/tested value | Conditions / limitation; contextual choice by reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| RIR error F04 | Measurement estimate: signed underprediction 0.95 reps (CI .17–1.73), I² 97.9% | Not a tolerance band or absolute error. Review anchored scale, exercise/load and actual matched sets; choose conservative allowable effort range, no automatic bias correction. |
| RPE-based vs fixed load F06–07 | Intervention: 8 weeks 3 sessions/week, matched reps/sets; pooled strength MD +2.07 kg (CI −.32–4.46) | Helms RPE arm used 2% load adjustment per 0.5 RPE outside target range; percentage arm also used completion-triggered progression and missed-rep reductions. These are tested protocol components, not independently validated increments. Choose available load step, rep range and effort tolerance with reviewer based on habitual exposure, objectives and equipment; test over-/under-response. |
| Velocity loss F08 | Tested comparator ≤25% vs >25%; strength/hypertrophy different results | Sensor-dependent repetition stopping, not weekly set threshold or RIR equivalence; not MVP default. |
| Set autoregulation F13 | 2/4/6% back-off experiment (Packet 002 V13) | Alters realized volume, no longitudinal superiority; no universal percentage. |

## 6. Synthetic evaluation cases

These are deterministic conformance fixtures, not clinical scenarios, evidence of efficacy or numerical rules. All observations are synthetic; all policies referenced are pending review. The same preservation invariant applies to every row, including unchanged/declined/invalidated proposals. Never retroactively modify completed prescriptions or record unapproved changes as approved.

Action codes: **R** read-only review, clarification, advice or no change (no new prescription approval); **E** already-authorized execution choice (no additional approval, log actual deviation/choice separately); **W** proposed session/week change within existing block constraints (explicit athlete approval required, with current-head and restriction revalidation); **B** proposed block revision (explicit athlete approval required; preserve earlier block and rationale). Safety stop/defer and referral advice is R, not a new approved program, and must not wait for plan approval. Where a row lists multiple acceptable actions, apply the approval rule for the chosen action. Athlete's actual choice or refusal is recorded independently of what is prescribed.
