# Fitness Intelligence — J3B.3
## Practical running prescription and progression methodology

**Candidate J3-M03 revision 0.1 · 2026-11-25 · Pending independent human review**  
Repository: https://github.com/dkiv/fitness-intelligence  
Scope: experienced recreational hybrid athletes, with a separate entry/re-entry branch for limited recent running.  
Authority: documentation only; no approved evidence, registered method, released rule, implementation or athlete prescription.

## 1. Executive summary

M03 supplies running prescriptions and bounded adaptation proposals to the same planner that consumes M01 strength prescriptions and M02 block objectives. It is not a second planner or a physiological estimator. The block determines which outcomes matter, which exposures are protected and which changes may be proposed. M03 determines which specific run and target are defensible from actual running history, recent execution and contextual observations. M01 and M03 proposals are combined and checked against the same calendar and protected commitments before any approval.

Recommended initial decisions:

- Preserve productive familiar running exposure. Initialize from actual, running-specific history rather than lifting strength, historical peak mileage, a device's estimated VO2max or theoretical pace zones.
- For a consistent runner, select familiar easy, long and demanding work appropriate to the block. For a low-exposure or returning runner, first establish a manageable easy pattern. A reviewed introductory quality pathway exists even without prior threshold experience; it is not the same as progression of an existing quality session.
- Effort and conversation are useful first-class prescriptions. Use pace ranges from eligible comparable actual work; use HR only with credible reference and sensor context, and primarily for steady tasks. A secondary pace or HR cue cannot compel an athlete to exceed the intended effort or ignore symptoms.
- Use separate dimensions for weekly running minutes, frequency, long duration, demanding-session count, work minutes, repetition structure, intensity, and local lifting demand. Hold is a genuine option. Small increments are proposed administrative defaults, not validated safety thresholds. A positive change requires a specific objective and a credible current reference.
- Distinguish actual completed quality repetitions (which can be athlete-attested) from measured achieved interval pace (which needs actual aligned observations). Missing detail can block pace progression without blocking all coaching. A completed activity is not proof of all instructed work.
- Replace M02 B11 familiar-running-hold only after an independently reviewed M03 and an explicitly approved block envelope. A new method version does not retroactively authorize new work. Default to one positive running axis; consider a narrowly defined, explicitly authorized modest easy-duration plus small lower-body lifting progression rather than imposing an indiscriminate one-discipline rule.
- Maintain separate exposure adherence and outcome review. No session count guarantees running performance, no estimated aerobic capacity guarantees long-run tolerance, and no strength exposure guarantees strength retention.

The numerical profile in §7 refines all R01–R20 from J3A.3. The four synthetic programs in §9 exercise strength emphasis, running emphasis, balanced development, and low running history. They include a first quality exposure, confirmed but unmeasured interval completion, unknown execution, a joint feasible change, and explicit block revision. None is a personal recommendation.

### 2.3 Interface and authority

Input: current owner context revision, exact approved M02 block version and intent, approved calendar predecessor, as-of observation snapshot, M01 proposed changes and supporting eligible inputs, verified method/rule versions, evidence selection and constraints. Output: a running prescription candidate or alternatives, hold, targeted question, defer or named conflict. The joint planner chooses and validates a complete candidate. Only trusted athlete approval changes an active future prescription; execution within an already approved range does not change the prescription. A new restriction can require stopping affected activity immediately without waiting for approval, and invalidates incompatible proposals. Clinical assessment and return-to-training clearance are outside scope; never infer clearance from a wearable or a successful warm-up. New severe symptoms such as chest pain, fainting, severe unusual breathlessness, or acute significant injury warrant stopping and appropriate urgent care; persistent or worsening pain, recurrent exercise intolerance or suspected energy deficiency warrant qualified assessment. No automated diagnosis.

## 3. Practical prescription methodology — references, initialization and templates (M03a)

### 3.1 Observation eligibility and initial dose

Select facts *before* looking at improvement. Pin source account/owner, immutable observation IDs or snapshot values, occurrence and availability times, date/zone, modality and run subtype, distance and time convention, provenance and coverage. Exclude ambiguous duplicates, deleted or unsupported records, impossible values and observations recorded after the decision's as-of time. Treadmill, trail, road and run/walk are distinct when relevant; keep other sports and lifting as context, never silently as running minutes. Split recordings of one outing may be merged only with verified identity; separate distinct sessions from days. A GPS recording can establish actual recorded movement but not automatically the approved logical slot, intended purpose or subjective tolerance. Ask the athlete when that distinction matters. Current restrictions, interruptions, changed shoes/surface/equipment, recent novel work and environmental conditions qualify applicability. Old bests serve as goal context, not current anchors.

Construct recent 3–4 representative completed weeks (prefer last 28 days) and retain each observed vector: running days, sessions, task minutes including warm-up/recovery/cool-down, running versus walking minutes if known, distance, longest session/distance, easy/long/quality purpose, demanding work minutes if known, actual status and tolerability. For time prescriptions, count active planned/actual session minutes including intentional recovery walking; exclude unrelated idle or commuting time; track elapsed test time separately. Do not use moving time for one reference and elapsed for another without qualification. Unknown quantities remain unknown. Use median and range as summaries, not fabricate a composite week from unrelated maxima. Select a representative actual week/structure near central exposure, corroborated with other weeks. Check frequency and duration separately; a week with omitted runs is not a confirmed low dose. Keep partial weeks separate; do not annualize or extrapolate. Confirm current regime, not merely last 28 dates. A low-frequency comparable reference may extend to 56 days with reason and current continuity (align M01 N01); never use that as a physiological expiry date.

| Running-specific branch | Starting selection (illustrative, conditional) | Fallback/entry |
|---|---|---|
| Consistent experienced runner | Keep familiar actual frequency, weekly duration/distance and purposes when productive, tolerable and feasible; choose representative existing longest duration, actual quality structure if objective warrants. | If strength priorities or availability change, retain protected purposes and reduce discretionary work only with reason and approved bounds. |
| Experienced hybrid, modest running | Typical actual 2–3 runs, perhaps 20–45 min each if demonstrated; preserve frequency, repeat easy work; long is relative to familiar tasks, not a mandatory weekly fraction. | A 2-run week may be all easy; quality entry only if appropriate, not required by an archetype label. |
| Experienced lifter, sparse running | General fitness is not running-specific exposure. If credible recent short run/walk tolerance, e.g. two 15–20 min easy opportunities; no demanding work, no forced continuous run or long session. | If shorter actual tolerance choose shorter, or qualitative familiar walking/run-walk option after confirming plausibility. If no defensible baseline or concerning symptoms, defer numeric running prescription. |
| Returning after interruption | Establish reason, retained actual work, recent comparable exposure and current restrictions; re-anchor to recent manageable work, not peak mileage; use fewer/shorter familiar easy sessions if tolerance unclear. | No catch-up, no automatic percentage loss/restart, no elapsed-time clearance. A changed floor requires explicit M02 review. |
| Incomplete coverage | Show observed lower bound and unknown interval; ask about representative actual frequency, longest recent outing, purpose, interruptions and tolerance. | Athlete-confirmed history can establish a tentative starting repeat; no automatic escalation on inferred/unknown history. Ask only for facts that change decision. |

Starting exposures can be lower than representative actual training where a reason exists. Do not use a generic 80% restart rule. Do not arbitrarily add a long run, speedwork or frequency just because the athlete is experienced at resistance training. A running-emphasis block with uncertain tolerance can reserve time and state future development intent while current week stays introductory. If exposure cannot serve the stated objective in its timeframe, surface the tradeoff rather than fake a performance guarantee.

### 3.2 Intensity references and target selection

Define a consistent anchored running effort scale (0 rest, 2 easy, 3–4 comfortable/moderate-easy, 6–7 controlled hard, 8 hard but repeatable, 10 maximal). Distinguish work-step RPE, session RPE (overall session, reported at a consistent time) and optional speech cue. Do not use a session RPE as the effort of every interval or equate lifting RPE/RIR with running effort. Ranges are coaching cues, not measured physiological boundaries.

| Anchor | Eligibility and derivation | Use / conflict behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Effort and speech | Explain scale and prompt; ask whether full sentences were comfortable on easy/long or demanding work was controlled/repeatable. | Default effort 2–4 and conversation for easy; controlled 6–7, faster 7–8. If easy target feels too hard, slow/walk or shorten. Not a lactate threshold measurement. |
| Easy pace | Last 2–3 comparable actual easy tasks, similar course/surface, time convention, conditions, purpose and confirmed comfort. Use observed representative range (round outward to practical seconds/km) not best pace. | Example 5:52, 5:58, 6:05/km -> optional 5:50–6:10/km, nominal 6:00. If conditions changed, effort governs and pace becomes descriptive. One qualified anchor supports familiar repeat, not faster prescription. |
| Achieved quality pace | Actual work-step durations and distances or comparable measured laps, correct alignment, similar structure, effort, terrain and timing. Prefer 2 confirmed comparable sessions. Use actual controlled representative pace range with explicit unit. | Example repeated 6-min work at 4:48–4:54/km -> 4:45–4:55/km optional; do not derive interval pace from total workout pace. When pace conflicts with intended repeatability or effort cap, slow, reduce or stop; do not force pace. |
| Race/field test | Dated measured task/distance, elapsed time, conditions, actual intent, current applicability. | Performance reference and specificity cue; no universal conversion to threshold pace or intervals. E.g. 50:00/10 km = 5:00/km for that task, not automatically threshold. Avoid extrapolating old PR. |
| Observed HR | Credible sensor/mode, context, time alignment and steady comparable task, plausible value. Optional reference band from observed HR at conversational effort; qualify in heat, hills, fatigue, medication, sensor change. | Example eligible easy mean HR 140, 144, 146 bpm on similar runs; optional descriptive steady cue 140–150 bpm if actual eligible steady values justify it. Whole-run mean alone cannot establish instantaneous range; use descriptive averages if no steady data. Not mandatory, not a cancellation trigger. |
| %HRmax | Credible dated mode-specific maximal/near-maximal effort and sensor provenance; use explicit athlete-selected zone definitions and version. A highest observed HR is not guaranteed true max. | Apply only agreed formula. E.g. 70% of a credible 190 bpm anchor is 133 bpm, not automatically easy or LT. No age-predicted exact zones. |
| HR reserve | Credible HRmax and resting HR under compatible documented method; `HRtarget = HRrest + fraction*(HRmax−HRrest)`. Version both anchors. | 70% HRR with max 190 and rest 50 is 148 bpm, not 133. Same percentage does not mean same zone. No exact intensity mapping. |
| LTHR | Dated threshold protocol or credible field estimate, actual effort, measurement method and limitations; specify which threshold/zone system. | Use athlete-approved range only when anchor eligible. Device estimate may be stale or protocol-unknown; do not silently recompute from a race or substitute HRmax. |
| Critical speed / laboratory thresholds | Multiple eligible maximal time-distance efforts with fit and uncertainty for CS; documented lactate/gas-exchange protocol for physiological threshold. | Optional specialist anchors; not required. CS is model-specific and not identical to LT/VT. No inferred exact boundary from a single race or everyday HR. |

Reference freshness: prefer current comparable regime and last 28 days; when less frequent use a relevant older reference only with explicit justification, stable conditions and current tolerance. Reassess after illness/interruption, major training or environment change, sensor replacement, material discordance or changed objective. Store dates and protocol, not a single mutable zone label. No need to test max HR or threshold merely to prescribe an easy run. If uncertain use effort/conversation and familiar duration; if pace eligibility absent do not propose faster numbers. Pace bounds in seconds/km are ascending (fast endpoint numerically smaller); if distance and pace are both binding compute worst-case duration at slow endpoint, including recovery/setup. Time-based tasks default for initial coach. HR lag and signal variability make HR targets poor primary cues for short repetitions; use repeatable effort and measured pace only if appropriate. No target must override safety or the athlete's allowed stop/slow choice. If an explicitly hard pace/effort combination is impossible, mark deviation and propose revision rather than quietly redefining the purpose.

Keep five fields separate in each review: (1) prescribed target and version, (2) intended session/step purpose, (3) actual achieved work, (4) actual reported effort and timing/scale, (5) measured physiological response and method. A planned zone selector is not an achieved zone. Maintain session-purpose counts, work minutes by intended/confirmed category (label which), and HR time-in-zone only with historical definitions, time-aligned valid values and eligible denominator. Report unknown time separately. Majority easy is a starting design choice, not a hard 80/20 ratio; a week with one quality session out of three does not imply one third high-intensity minutes.

### 3.3 Candidate template library

All numeric ranges here are provisional programming choices for appropriately eligible athletes; pick a concrete value and bounds based on history, not a random sample from the range. Do not introduce all templates into a week. Count work, intentional warm-up, recovery, cool-down in session active minutes. Extra route/setup minutes are separate calendar reservations. For continuous easy runs, first/last minutes can be slower within total, not extra unless explicitly listed. A duration range must be compatible with the entire weekly envelope at both endpoints. Use an exact nominal plus separately approved execution flexibility (e.g., ability to slow, walk briefly, stop; fixed planned minutes if weekly floors/ceilings require). Shortening outside permitted duration is a recorded deviation, not a rewrite. Stop for concerning symptoms regardless of prescription. Examples are not optimal intervals, race predictions or physiological thresholds.

For quality tasks do not prescribe a target merely because the week has an empty slot. Use role-specific purpose, actual familiar structure and calendar. Work-rest relationship is a design parameter, not a universal recovery interval. Recovery running may count toward total running exposure but not demanding work minutes; if recovery is walking, record walk vs run separately where possible. A route that requires waiting or crossing may invalidate pace comparison; not all slow pace is deterioration. For long runs, discuss practical access to fluids and appropriate familiar fueling; use P004/007 qualitative nutrition guidance without estimating glycogen, energy availability or prescribing an unreviewed nutritional method.

### 3.4 Entry to quality without prior quality history

This is a positive change from zero demanding work, not a special case of the +1–2 min advanced progression rule (zero to six minutes cannot meet that rule). A separately reviewed entry role and athlete-approved M02 envelope are required. Provisional gate: at least 3 representative weeks of consistent manageable running, normally ≥2 sessions/week with ≥6 confirmed outings overall, including two recent continuous easy sessions of about 25–30 min at conversational effort (or a specifically reviewed comparable task when continuous running is not the objective). No current unresolved restriction, material adverse running response or conflicting lower-body commitment. Current objective benefits from introductory quality, athlete wants it, calendar permits preparation and recovery, and sufficient actual feedback indicates running-specific tolerance. Three weeks/six outings and durations are programming judgments, not injury thresholds. They do not prove readiness or require immediate quality introduction. Override only with explicit reviewed alternative appropriate to current history; a longer established history of easy running can qualify, a weightlifting PR cannot. If run/walk tolerance only, maintain or develop easy running first; don't automatically convert walking to running under the quality label.

Candidate first Q0: 3×2 min controlled work, RPE 5–6, two 2-min easy recoveries, 10 min easy at each end, total 30. Replace a familiar 30-min easy session, retain weekly minutes/frequency, count one demanding session and six work minutes for the conservative demand ledger even if effort is below threshold. Do not simultaneously increase long duration, total time or lower-body load/sets under the joint default. If 30 min not familiar, select a specifically reviewed shorter structure or wait. During session, athlete may slow or convert remaining work to easy, or stop; if completed work is unknown, do not escalate. Repeat the introductory structure until two eligible manageable exposures plus representative week and relevant next-session responses; then consider continued Q0 or a reviewed controlled-quality transition at fixed work duration and no additional intensity/duration rise in the same review. For instance 3×2 at 6–7 instead of 5–6 is an intensity-axis change (requires role authorization and confirmed tolerance), then later work increase. No mandatory advancement into faster intervals. A block that prohibits quality needs revision; reserved time is not entry authority.

## 4. Running progression and execution review (M03b)

### 4.1 Evidence eligibility is action-specific

Distinguish completed, partial, missed (explicitly confirmed), and unknown logical slots, and record actual exposure even when outside the prescription. Distinguish an athlete's confirmation of a rep from device-measured pace. A provider summary may establish an outing's actual duration/distance when attributed and valid; it does not automatically establish completion of every planned step. Inference from instructions is ineligible for quality progression. An explicit athlete report of all reps at prescribed work duration and appropriate effort can support duration progression without exact pace; a claim of measured pace requires corresponding actual distance/time or credible measured lap records. No mandatory FIT or HRV. A single reported session RPE on a quality run cannot establish each rep's effort. Optional brief questions: “Did you complete all three 6-minute efforts, with the two recoveries? Were the last efforts still controlled?” and, where relevant, “Any unusual leg soreness or problems on the next lift?” Ask only if the answer changes a decision.

| Action | Minimum positive eligibility (in addition to objective, feasible calendar and bounds) | If insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Repeat familiar easy | Credible actual recent similar duration and running context; no known adverse restriction. RPE optional. | Conservative repeat if credible; clarify missing actual dose or defer exact prescription. |
| Increase easy minutes | Two recent comparable attributable easy exposures at current nominal duration/role with confirmed completion and credible manageability (reported conversational comfort, anchored RPE, or targeted qualitative report), representative week and no contradictory local response. Summary plus athlete confirmation sufficient. | Hold or ask. No imputed RPE from HR. One strong observation can justify safety reduction, not default positive change. |
| Increase long minutes | Two comparable actual long outings at current duration, credible conditions/effort and acceptable during/after tolerance; confirm recent representative week and distance novelty where possible; check subsequent relevant lift/run. | Hold or choose familiar shorter exposure; unknown longest recent run or changed route -> clarify or avoid novel extension. |
| Increase quality work minutes/reps | Two current-structure exposures with actual completed repetitions/work duration (athlete-attested or measured), appropriate work effort/repeatability, known recovery deviations, relevant subsequent tolerance and weekly exposure. | Keep familiar structure if credible, ask, or reduce on difficulty. Instruction/split aggregate alone cannot satisfy. |
| Increase pace (faster sec/km) | Above quality gate plus aligned measured achieved work pace/time-distance (not instruction), comparable task/environment, stable effort and actual demonstrated faster controlled work or explicit justified specificity; reviewed pace step. | Hold pace or effort-only; no HR-only conversion. |
| Add/redistribute frequency | At least two representative completed weeks at current frequency with credible tolerance, athlete preference/availability, viable redistributed session lengths, no shortening protected task. | Hold frequency, address adherence or clarify. Do not count missing import as low frequency. |
| Introduce first quality | Q0 gate §3.4, explicit entry authorization, not ordinary quality progression. | Repeat manageable easy pattern, ask or defer. |
| Reduce or move | Credible relevant issue (effort, fatigue, route, time, symptoms, priority or actual availability); no need for two observations to act conservatively. | If uncertain and no safety issue hold/ask; stop affected work for concerning symptoms regardless of data. |

The two-exposure preference is a conservative administrative default, not an individual adaptation time constant. Count distinct actual eligible sessions at the comparable current nominal structure, not two imported copies or an old PR. Same-role references can be from prior block if current regime and role unchanged; after interruption re-evaluate. One completed session need not trigger positive adjustment. Scheduled phase changes are still subject to current constraints and the eligibility of that explicitly reviewed rule, not an automatic exception. RPE-free progression requires actual work plus credible qualitative tolerance; no universal questionnaire. For easy tasks summary-level observations plus one brief confirmation spanning both sessions may suffice. For quality duration a report can attest all work durations, count, control and relevant recovery changes; no need to require exact bpm or sec/km. For pace, summary-only history insufficient. If actual completion is inferred, mark descriptive only unless independently confirmed. A new restriction supersedes routine eligibility.

### 4.2 Execution-to-decision matrix

| Observation | Acceptable next decisions | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| Completed familiar work at expected effort | Hold if productive, or propose one eligible objective-directed change within caps. | Success demands progression. |
| Easier than expected on similar task | Confirm comparability and actual work. Consider hold, longer duration or a reviewed pace change according to purpose. | Exact threshold or VO2max improvement. |
| Easy run at RPE 6, same pace | Slow/walk now, shorten if needed; inspect heat, route, sleep/fueling, leg work, symptoms; hold progression or reduce next affected run. | Global overtraining, precise recovery deficit, causal attribution. |
| Faster easy pace at similar comfort | Record observed pace difference; keep easy cue, repeat to check direction. | Prescribe every easy run faster, infer economy. |
| Slower quality pace but all reps controlled | If heat, hill, wind, stops or different route, effort may be appropriate; hold/qualify pace; compare within context. | Failure of physiological adaptation solely from pace. |
| Only 2 of 3 intervals completed | Ask why if not clear: time, route, effort, pain or ambiguity. If exertion limited, remove one rep or lower pace next time; if logistics, preserve familiar structure when feasible. | Count three because workout was marked completed; diagnose lack of fitness. |
| Interval instructions + aggregate splits only | Report prescribed work, actual outing duration/distance and unknown rep execution. Ask if completed or hold; no duration/pace escalation on that evidence alone. | Expand aggregate count to chronological achieved reps or use instructed pace as achieved. |
| RPE missing, actual easy run verified and athlete reports comfortable | Eligible qualitative manageability if context fits; no imputed numerical RPE. | Missing equals low effort or adherence. |
| HR implausible or cadence lock suspected | Ignore untrustworthy HR target/metric; use effort and eligible time/pace; ask about sensor or corroborate if material. | Diagnose artifact from a single mean, or update threshold automatically. |
| Time or route unavailable | Move within purpose/date constraints or propose shorter familiar task; record actual shortfall; no repayment debt. | Shortfall is necessarily physiological. |
| Repeated higher effort or poorer comparable output | Verify conditions/coverage, examine local/general burden, restrictions and actual exposure; hold/reduce/redistribute; M02 review for persistent protected outcome difficulty. | Proof of inadequate stimulus or a quantified interference penalty. |
| Pain worsening, concerning symptoms, new restriction | Stop/defer affected work and seek appropriate professional guidance; preserve unrelated safe work only if appropriate. Review block if floor infeasible. | Athlete approval or prior success overrides health restriction. |

### 4.3 Concrete change operators

Select a specific objective-directed operator; keep all other demanding axes fixed except mechanically necessary effects (e.g. extending work adds total time and recovery if explicitly planned, but no independent easy/long extension). Time-based running is the default because it is easy to prescribe and measure; preserve separate distance bounds where specified. A run may be time-based or distance-based, never treat both as exact unless feasible and explicitly compatible. A predicted distance from a pace band is a planning range, not achieved distance. If a hard distance limit cannot be bounded, use an enforceable distance stop/route with known distance, or return conflict.

| Operator | Proposed nominal change | Constraints and examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hold | Same purpose, frequency, structure and targets | Productive exposure and sufficient observations; if eligibility uncertain, repeat only credible familiar work; disclose uncertainty. |
| Easy duration | +5 min on one ordinary easy task, smaller when caps bind | 30→35 min if weekly and role bounds allow. At 40 weekly min cap 4 min; select 2–4 min or hold rather than round to 5. RPE unchanged. |
| Long duration | +5 min on existing long task; +10 only specifically approved and familiar context supports | 65→70, same intended easy effort, no additional quality rise. Inspect expected distance and longest recent run. No universal long-run fraction. |
| Frequency redistribution | +1 easy session, keep weekly nominal minutes initially | 2×30 → 3×20 if no protected 30-min task; or split one discretionary 40 into 20+20 while retaining long run. Additional starts/days can change local burden; not “free” progress. |
| Work duration | +1–2 total work minutes in one quality session | 2×6→2×7 (+2), 4×2→4×2:30 (+2), or 3×5→3×5:30 (+1:30). Prefer uniform repetition duration when simple; alternatively extend last rep with deliberate purpose and clearly encode it. No speed increase. |
| Repetition count | +1 familiar rep only if work-minute cap met; include extra between-rep recovery | 4×1→5×1 (+1 work and +1 recovery if 1-min recovery). Adding a 6-min rep violates default +2 even if time fits; hold or reviewed larger role-specific step. |
| Target intensity | Keep work duration/count/rest and weekly time; change pace or effort only if explicitly reviewed | Optional pace step at most 5 s/km faster from credible demonstrated pace; narrower block cap wins. Maintain intended effort cap, no automatic pace progression. Changing purpose from easy to Q0 uses entry rule, not this operator. |
| Reduce | −5–10 min affected easy/long, −1 work rep or smaller duration/pace; replace some/all quality with familiar easy if authorized | Retain warm-up/cool-down as appropriate; greater reduction or stop if warranted. Floor conflict routed to block, not force minimum execution. |
| Move | Same nominal purpose and dose in feasible future window | Preserve fixed dates and adjacency constraints; approval unless already authorized flexibility. No double sessions as default repayment. |
| Defer/clarify | No positive change while a material prerequisite is unknown | Provide targeted question or explicit conflict, not invented capacity. |

Recovery and warm-up changes count in total time. Do not shorten recoveries to squeeze an otherwise infeasible progression; reducing recovery is a separate demanding axis requiring reviewed permission. No automatic increase in pace from successful completion. Uniform structures often more practical to execute and verify; if the smallest uniform whole-minute change exceeds cap, use 30-sec work steps or a smaller supported structure. Use exact integer seconds and target endpoint checks; no rounding up beyond a cap. A role-specific reviewed larger work increment can be useful for established longer threshold sessions (e.g. +3 min total) but not a silent exception; update profile and M02 envelope before proposal. Faster repetitions are controlled, not all-out. Do not increase both work duration and intensity, nor add quality frequency and work intensity automatically.

## 5. Concurrent scheduling, recovery and joint proposals (M03c)

### 5.1 Inspect local overlap rather than a cross-sport score

Review recent actual running by purpose, longest exposure and changes; recent lower-body movement loading, sets, reps and effort, unusual eccentric/novel work and other sports; upcoming protected strength/run slots and events; optional athlete feedback about local legs vs generalized difficulty. Calendar search covers previous/next two local days plus adjacent week/block boundaries (M01 N16/M02 B16); expand for relevant event or dense schedule. Check exact approved spacing (UTC elapsed interval when required, with DST handling), not a physiological rule inferred from days. Moving a session can help protect quality but does not prove cause or required recovery duration. A short easy run and demanding threshold task have different practical overlap with heavy squat. Upper-body progression is not automatically barred by running. Optional sleep/resting HR/verified HRV can prompt a question if material, not automatically cancel a workout; unavailable or unverified HRV never required. No HRV/readiness score, ACWR, summed tonnage-km score or individual interference coefficient.

| Context | Candidate preference |
|---|---|
| Strength emphasis | Retain protected run purposes/long capability; prioritize specific lift work and avoid unnecessary competing lower-body demand. A small easy extension may fit if running objective warrants, not obligatory. |
| Running emphasis | Preserve agreed lift specificity/floors and assess performance; retain familiar load/sets when productive, consider optional accessory reduction/move before compromising protected strength. Prefer key running development when priorities allow but no unilateral removal of protected lift. |
| Balanced | Hold both if productive; selectively progress a justified task, allow independently feasible upper-body progression or reviewed modest joint combination; ask athlete on tied meaningful tradeoff. |
| Local leg fatigue | Slow/reduce/move relevant run or adjust M01 lower-body accessory/load locally; preserve unaffected upper-body work. If protected session would be lost, surface conflict. |
| Generalized difficulty | Check restrictions, illness symptoms, broad burden, sleep/fueling context when actionable; consider coordinated reduction or block review; do not diagnose from sparse observations. |

### 5.2 Joint change classification and validation

M03 receives M01 proposed delta with exact role, movement, predecessor and initial baseline. M01 independently satisfies N01–N17 and M02 B07–10. Classify running changes by positive axis: easy minutes, long minutes, frequency, run fraction, demanding work, quality count/entry, intensity, shorter recovery/novel structure. One operator may mechanically increase total time; report both, not two independent choices. Classify lower-body changes: external load, repetitions, set count, novel role/effort, plus context of actual workload. Classify upper-body separately; account for time and general burden. Do not hide a load rise by removing unrelated work, or count redistribution as zero burden just because total minutes unchanged. At most one positive independent running axis by default; M02 B12 remains binding until explicitly amended.

| Pair of changes | Candidate treatment |
|---|---|
| Quality duration/intensity/entry or long extension + any positive lower-body demand | Default reject combined; select priority, hold alternative or explicitly reviewed planned combination with detailed feasibility and evidence. No automatic exception. |
| Ordinary easy + small familiar lower-body load/rep rise | Default B12 rejects if unchanged block. Proposed optional joint profile `J-EASY-SMALL` below permits only narrowly bounded combination after independent method review AND athlete-approved envelope. |
| Frequency redistribution, same total minutes + lower-body positive | Not automatically safe; default choose one. Optional separately approved planned combination only with verified distribution/spacing/tolerance and no protected purpose loss. |
| Running progression + upper-body progression | Independently feasible may coexist if M01/M03 gates, overall time, restrictions, block rules and relevant burden permit. No mandatory reduction merely for coexistence. |
| Two substantial demanding rises (e.g., +quality work and +squat set, faster intervals + heavier squat) | Reject default joint proposal, even if each independently fits or net time unchanged. Consider separate reviews; explicit planned exception requires human coaching review and exact bounds. |
| Hold/negative delta in one, positive in another | Not automatically accepted: validate protected floors/outcomes and why reduction is warranted. No trading away a hard secondary commitment. |

`J-EASY-SMALL` proposed exception (programming judgment, not empirical validation): one ordinary easy session +≤5 min (and weekly cap applies), no long, frequency, run-fraction, quality or intensity increase; one familiar lower-body working group change only, either +1 total rep under N04 OR smallest equipment step ≤2.5% (narrower than N06's usual 5%); no sets, role novelty, prescribed effort rise or other lower-body positive group. Both independently eligible, recent representative joint weeks and two relevant manageable exposures per changed role, no unresolved local fatigue/deterioration or meaningful unknown relevant commitment, calendar and protection intact at worst permitted endpoints. Block explicitly names this profile and exact allowed dimensions. If absent, use B12 default; do not claim a later M03 version grants it. Athlete may prefer not to use exception. Larger combinations require named, reviewed role-specific profile and block revision; not part of initial automatic candidate set.

Joint procedure: generate each discipline's hold and bounded alternatives, construct feasible combinations, reject any hard constraint, then compare ranked objectives and material tradeoffs. If one development priority outranks the other, choose compatible proposal that serves it while preserving protected commitments; when both currently productive, prefer hold over unjustified increases. If a meaningful choice between tied goals remains, present options, don't use stable ID to rank athlete priorities. Soft preferences and local overlap guide equally meaningful options; minimize unnecessary changes and burden. Return named conflict (IDs, requested vs available values, affected slots/outcomes, alternatives including block review) if no feasible combination. Never silently turn a protected threshold into an easy run, delete a protected squat or change a fixed date. Read-only advice, execution choice, weekly prescription change, block revision and context/goal revision remain distinct.

## 6. M02 running envelope and outcome review (M03c)

### 6.1 Explicit replacement of B11, not implicit authorization

M02 rev 0.1 B11 defaults to familiar running hold with zero automatic rise. Reserved time is calendar capacity, not a prescription. The following is a proposed B11 extension to be jointly reviewed; do not alter current M02 records or active blocks. First authorize an exact M03 rule/profile and evidence policy through normal review; then propose a new or revised block envelope and obtain explicit athlete approval. Only after that can the joint planner propose changed weekly prescriptions under those bounds; those prescriptions still require approval. A block revision covers affected future detailed weeks (replace or explicitly withdraw) and leaves started days/recorded outcomes/history intact. Old blocks remain running-hold unless revised. A stricter B12 or protection rule wins over M03's defaults. Planned entry/transition roles need explicit bounds and timing; no automatic expansion at next week.

Maintain separate nominal and high/low endpoint validations. For a new positive weekly duration Wnew, default `Wnew − Wp ≤ min(600, floor(0.10×Wp), block_week_step)` in seconds; also `Wnew ≤ min(W0+1200, block_abs_ceiling)`. Check maximum allowed totals, including any widened execution range, against corresponding high-end predecessor change and cumulative cap; minimum totals against floors. Compare actual history H for eligibility, never use actual missed minutes to inflate authorized rise or reset W0. At Wp=40 min, step ≤4 min; at 140, ≤10 min; at 200, ≤10 min. For Wp=0, percentage cap permits zero, so use separately reviewed initial/re-entry prescription based on credible actual tolerance rather than calling it progression. For Q0 entry from D0=0, explicit entry bounds replace D0+4 illustration, not a hidden exception. A reduction may exceed positive step caps for safety; if proposed below hard floor, return block conflict. No resetting cumulative baseline at every week, moving slots or relabeling roles. A new approved block may intentionally re-anchor to current credible history, not launder unreviewed increases.

For demanding work, default `Dnew−Dp ≤120 s` and `Dnew≤D0+240 s` where D0>0 and actual approved role caps allow; Q0 and explicit assessment or role transition have separate reviewed limits. Include extra recoveries in Wnew. If pace/distance-based intervals, calculate time bounds and only propose a change if all relevant quantities can be bounded; otherwise time/effort alternative. For frequency increase `Fnew≤min(Fp+1,F0+1,approved_ceiling)` and keep W nominal fixed initially; any added session must fit both day and session constraints and role lower limits. For long role `Lnew−Lp≤300 s` default and `Lnew≤L0+600 s` illustrative. Do not interpret a +10% administrative limit as Frandsen's association; no universal safe progression rate. Relative and absolute caps restrict candidate search, not injury probability. For smaller exposures use smaller steps rather than forcing +5. Larger trained-runner steps might be reasonable but require reviewed configuration and context. All ceilings are maximum permitted proposals, not targets to reach.

### 6.2 Outcome-specific review, not physiological inference

| Outcome | Eligible comparison and interpretation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Easy running performance | Same route/surface, duration and time convention, comparable effort/conditions; compare pace at similar effort, or HR at similar steady pace with validated protocol/sensor. Report actual values, dates, context. | Faster pace at comfortable effort is an observed task difference, not measured economy/VO2max. Mean HR not necessarily steady HR. |
| Long-duration capability | Comparable long task, duration/distance completed, speech/effort, conditions/fueling, interruptions and after-effects. | Stable weekly minutes or short easy pace does not establish long endurance; no drift estimate from aggregate split totals. |
| Controlled quality performance | Same actual work/recovery structure, measured work pace where eligible, effort/repeatability, task conditions and after-effects; count completed work independently. | Faster total workout pace not interval pace. Completion without actual pace can establish work adherence but not pace improvement. |
| Race or timed field outcome | Dated measured distance, elapsed time, comparable course, conditions, effort, preparation/freshness; optionally ordinary controlled test. | No mandatory maximal testing; Hurst error not a personal change cutoff. No exact physiological threshold inferred. |
| Secondary strength | M01 comparable movement/equipment/load/reps/set role/effort, adherence and actual outcome direction. | Set counts or estimated 1RM not measured max or muscle mass; temporal association with running not causal proof. |

Apply Packet 009: define eligible task before viewing result, preserve denominator/exclusions, use ordered raw observations plus recent range/median where meaningful, compare against pinned objective reference and recent current regime. Label observed improvement/deterioration, stable within available resolution, mixed, or insufficient comparable evidence. A one-off difference is not sustained direction. A repeated result may justify practical review without formal significance. Report separate exposure adherence status and outcome direction per objective; unknown even when all slots completed. Compare within actual task, not across different efforts, terrain or device conventions. If repeated comparable secondary deterioration or changed feasibility, invoke M02 B15 review; one material adverse observation prompts immediate clarification/hold; serious restrictions immediate stop/defer. Use agreed functional review tolerance as trigger (not statistical MDC or guarantee). If no suitable test, report unknown and optionally schedule familiar assessment opportunity. End of block can continue productive program, extend with approval, transition, or revise goals; do not force taper/deload/max test. Preserve Packet 010 rolling horizon and interruption principles: missed work is not debt, don't restore peak mileage automatically, confirm current retained actual exposure and restrictions before re-entry. Past patterns are tentative context, not causal personal coefficients.

Action preference is not “always progress when possible.” Generate positive candidates only for a specific objective rationale (e.g., long-run capability target not currently attained but familiar exposure is manageable, or planned quality development). Otherwise retain a productive dose. If an athlete prefers faster quality but lower-body protection makes it infeasible, preserve protection and show alternate schedule or explicit tradeoff. A missing optional sensor never invalidates a defensible effort prescription. A materially changed source observation invalidates a pending proposal even if rounded totals happen to match; historical replay uses pinned old facts, not a silent refresh. A changed restriction may make an already approved future session inappropriate; flag rather than silently overwrite. For safety, an athlete can stop immediately; any future plan change still uses trusted approval.
