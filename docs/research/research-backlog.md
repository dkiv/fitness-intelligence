## RT-02 follow-up

[Packet 002](002-working-set-volume-adjustment.md#7-research-backlog) completed the targeted preliminary appraisal. Its proposed review order starts with the female Enes version of record, followed by the Barsuhn full paper, the Pelland model and then the candidate decision policy.

| ID | Priority / status | Question and next action |
|---|---|---|
| RT-02a | P0 — full-text extracts, verify | Confirm Enes male and female version-of-record protocol schedules, cumulative sets, actual exposure, participant flow, strength contrast estimates and test reliability. Obtain supplementary raw data where accessible. |
| RT-02b | P0 — gap | Is escalation beneficial independently of total assigned dose? Seek directly dose-matched trajectory trials; if unavailable, retain gap and design prospective comparisons. |
