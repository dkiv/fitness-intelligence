# Research library

A research packet records what a research session found, what it could check, and which questions remain open. A reviewed J1 scientific claim describes a specific result in a defined population, comparison, outcome and time horizon. A programming policy describes a choice the coach could make. Accepting a claim does not establish when a planner should act on it.

### Scientific review blockers

- Map overlapping primary studies in Schumann, Petré and Huiberts and across the running reviews. Verify interaction estimates for timing, training status, sex and modality. A significant result in one subgroup and an uncertain result in another cannot establish a difference between them.
- Keep maximal strength, explosive strength, biopsy-measured fiber hypertrophy and whole-muscle hypertrophy distinct. Verify the contributing populations, muscle groups, measures and intervals for each contrast.
- Separate running economy at a specified speed or fatigue state, time to exhaustion and race or time-trial performance. Determine habitual lifting exposure before inferring an incremental benefit for runners who already strength train.
- Check the duration of each contributing comparison against its draft scope. For example, C11 reports 6–14 weeks for the high-load running-economy comparison, while `high-load-running-economy@1` uses the overall 6–24-week horizon. Check the Petré timing strata and Llanos-Lagos performance contrasts separately. Leave the proposed records intact for adjudication.
- Obtain the primary reports for abstract-based results, including Gomes, Støren, Trowell and Zanini. Verify direct between-group contrasts, intervals, realized training and baseline experience. Confirm versions of record, correction notices, cohort overlap and reuse rights. The packet’s [human review checklist](003-concurrent-strength-running.md#8-human-review-checklist) records source-specific checks and the outstanding programming and evaluation decisions.

### Nutrition MVP product decision

Fitness Intelligence will prioritize Hevy and Garmin. Hevy supplies strength history. Subject to verification of **historical coverage**, Garmin is the intended source for daily calorie intake, body-weight observations, estimated energy expenditure, endurance and other activity history, and recovery observations. Macronutrient and meal-level ingestion are optional extensions. Health Connect and additional nutrition integrations are deferred unless missing observations demonstrably limit coaching usefulness.

**Policy-review clarification for NU-P02, NU-P03 and NU-P04.** Review the inputs for each decision separately.

| Decision | What the observations support |
| --- | --- |
| Generate a target | A reviewed method could use workload, goals, preferences and any inputs its units require to recommend daily protein or carbohydrate, or fueling for a particular session. Missing macro logs alone do not preclude that recommendation. |
| Describe observed intake | Report the available calories, nutrient amounts or meal timing with their provenance and completeness. Voluntary athlete feedback can add context, such as a meal before a run or tolerance of a fueling option. |
| Assess adherence | Compare a target only with corresponding observations over the relevant interval. Without observed macro amounts, whether the athlete consumed the recommended amounts cannot be verified automatically. |
| Infer physiological adequacy | Calorie intake and weight alone cannot establish nutritional adequacy, glycogen status or adherence. Stable weight also leaves energy availability unresolved. |

L1 (training context), L2 (daily calories and weight), L3 (daily macros) and L4 (meal timing and amounts) describe capabilities for a particular variable and time period. Athletes can provide meal timing voluntarily even when daily totals are unavailable. Progression through the classes is optional. Reviewers should apply this distinction to the archived policies and case expectations, including N22.

### Nutrition scientific review blockers

- Distinguish energy balance from energy availability (EA). Verify the exposure and comparator in low-EA studies. Stable weight cannot establish adequate EA. Sports medicine and dietetics reviewers must determine referral boundaries and assess corrected REDs guidance. Automated REDs diagnosis, clinical risk scoring and a universal EA threshold are outside scope.
- Check the measured endpoint and time horizon. Acute muscle protein synthesis does not establish chronic hypertrophy or strength gains. Glycogen restoration does not establish subsequent performance. Distinguish direct concurrent-training comparisons from transfer across disciplines or to athletes experienced in both.
- Extract Hartono’s protein study counts separately from pooled effects. Check the population, model and uncertainty of Morton’s breakpoint before interpreting it. A population estimate cannot establish an individual requirement.
- Check units, populations and conditions for consensus reference ranges. Review uncertainty in wearable expenditure and food logs, including which reference method and device version were used. A study-average error does not calibrate an individual record. Personalized total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) estimation remains a Packet 007 research question.
- Verify versions of record, corrections, reuse rights and overlapping cohorts, including reused Caldwell performance data and primary studies shared by reviews. The [human review checklist](004-integrated-nutrition-fueling.md#8-human-review-checklist) retains the source-specific extractions and clinical, methodology and evaluation gates.

### Running observations and engineering follow-up

The measurement appraisal describes canonical observations independently of the provider. Section 2.9 dates the mutable capability snapshot to 2026-11-24. Historical coverage, resolution and comparability determine which decisions a given record can support. | Decision | Observations and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Describe habitual exposure or compare runs | With verified coverage, dated activity summaries support distance, duration, frequency and longest-run exposure. Comparing performance also requires sufficiently similar tasks, effort, terrain and conditions. |
| Assess interval execution or historical intensity | B2 aggregate split rows can represent several splits. Performed-workout steps describe instructions. ## Numbering and revisions

Assign the next unused three-digit number to each new research question or packet of related questions. Keep its number and filename stable across revisions. Cite the packet number, revision, section and Git commit when reviewing an extraction or proposing a rule. Evidence IDs such as E02 are local to their packet. J1 references use exact revision keys such as `moesgaard@1`.

Keep the supplied revision in Git history. For a correction or follow-up extraction, increment the packet revision, date it, and record what changed and why. Update the associated draft and backlog when affected. A change to a previously released catalog record needs a new J1 revision key.

## From packet to reviewed evidence

1. Extract proposed records into a fragment under `drafts/` and keep their source locators, access depth, uncertainty and screening dispositions. Validate the fragment in the context of its full target manifest, including existing relations and withdrawals.
2. Assign an independent human reviewer. Use the review checklists in [Packet 001](001-strength-progression-periodization.md#8-human-review-checklist), [Packet 002](002-working-set-volume-adjustment.md#8-human-review-checklist), [Packet 003](003-concurrent-strength-running.md#8-human-review-checklist), [Packet 004](004-integrated-nutrition-fueling.md#8-human-review-checklist) and [Packet 005](005-running-development-measurement.md#8-human-review-checklist). Check each result against its source, including the population, comparator, direction, units, interval and limitations. Review publication notices, reuse rights, overlapping trials, scope terms, provisional quality and any argument for transfer. Record unresolved issues with the affected record.
3. Record the designated reviewer's identity, role, date and explicit decision on each accepted revision in version control. A claim requires reviewed supporting sources. Operational eligibility also requires a reviewed release. A separately authorized release receives a new immutable manifest, registry entry and digest under the [J1 release procedure](../scientific-evidence-catalog.md#review-and-release-procedure).

import json
from hashlib import sha256
from pathlib import Path

from fitness_intelligence.evidence import load_release

base = load_release("j1-screening-1").release

candidate = CorpusRelease.model_validate_json(data, strict=True)

assert tuple(len(getattr(candidate, group)) for group in groups) == (31, 50, 63, 4, 14, 0)
assert candidate.approval is None

catalog = Catalog(candidate, sha256(data).hexdigest())
for claim in candidate.claims:
    assert catalog.ineligibility(claim.key) == "pending"
    result = catalog.retrieve(claim.scope)
    assert result == catalog.retrieve(claim.scope)
    assert result.gaps and not result.matches

## From candidate policy to planner rule

Review each method separately from the empirical claims it cites. Identify which choices reflect evidence, athlete preferences or engineering safeguards. Define eligible observations, alternatives (including holding the dose), parameters, authority to change a plan and conditions for deferring a decision. A reviewer must justify any proposed numeric threshold or transfer to an experienced hybrid athlete.

Before adopting a rule version, exercise the [synthetic evaluation cases](001-strength-progression-periodization.md#6-evaluation-cases) with pinned block, evidence and rule versions. Check acceptable alternatives, missing feedback, secondary objectives, protected assessments and declined changes. Record the policy decision, review and validation results with any later planner change so historical plans can be replayed with their original rules. Prospective effectiveness is a separate research question. All five packets and their drafts remain research inputs. E1 continues to use its existing synthetic progression rules.
