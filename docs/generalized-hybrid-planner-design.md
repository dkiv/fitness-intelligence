# E4.0 · Generalized hybrid training planner

**Design for independent review.** The first useful increment takes an athlete’s confirmed recent training, ranked goals and calendar and produces one familiar, fully specified week inside a proposed multiweek block. The athlete can see why each session is there, what may vary during execution and which later decisions require another review. A subsequent increment can propose a small change when the relevant work and response are known. All examples and numerical defaults below are synthetic candidates for review. Operational authority requires the release and approval steps below.

For review, start with the [block contract](#the-block-an-athlete-approves), [progression and restoration decisions](#review-policy-and-the-four-reference-values), [authorization gates](#rule-and-evidence-authorization) or [worked weeks](#worked-synthetic-decisions).

## The block an athlete approves

| Contract | Required content and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Athlete context revision | Owner, effective and recorded instants, IANA planning zone, ranked objectives (ties allowed), measurement protocol and desired direction, horizon or event, priorities the athlete is willing to trade, experience and relevant current regime. Capture preferences separately from binding exclusions, dated restrictions, available windows, time budgets, equipment and discrete load options by location. Unknown fields carry a reason. Record who supplied each constraint, when it applies and whether it is mandatory. |
| Cycle | Stable owner-scoped cycle ID, horizon and ordered nonoverlapping block identities with local date boundaries. Begin with one block. Add a successor by an explicit proposal with a start date, new baseline evidence, review of the ending block and cross-boundary checks. |
| Block contract revision | Stable block ID, revision and predecessor, start, week count, phase intent by week, ranked objectives, protected secondary outcomes and exposure commitments, observation cutoff, initial baseline and sources, profile and allowed operators, recap/review dates and reasons to review early. Emphasis is a priority order (strength, running or balanced), never a mandatory split. |
| Protected outcome | Name the task, conditions, measurement, comparison and review trigger, e.g. preserve performance at a familiar squat test during a running block. Record baseline/follow-up or `unknown`. A decline or missing comparison can call for review. No validator can promise a performance result. |
| Exposure commitment | Name the logical purpose, frequency or bounds per period, meaningful minimum dose, fixed event or allowed placement windows, plus any approved substitution. For example, two weekly running opportunities including one continuous long outing. Both must survive every feasible candidate even if a frequency redistribution preserves total minutes. |
| Adaptation envelope | Named dose dimensions by movement/group and running role, units, per-review and cumulative bounds, allowed execution endpoints, discrete steps, effort caps, joint profile and protected floors. Running pins initial frequency, total time, longest role time and demanding work time/count. A fixed-running block retains its familiar dose until the athlete approves a revision granting a reviewed running envelope. Reserved time conveys no dose authority. |
| Week intent and prescription | Store an intent for each valid block week `1..N` (focus, protected opportunities, phase and conditions for change). Detail one upcoming seven-local-date week. At most one following week needs tentative placements for boundary review. Label later intent and tentative placements as provisional. A detailed prescription names its approved block revision, week, exact sessions, rationale and approval state. |
| Task identity | Allocate a stable logical session key once per occurrence within owner/cycle/block/week. A move or spelling change keeps the key. A replacement explicitly retires or maps a task prospectively. A recurring role identifies comparable tasks across weeks. A movement key plus variation, equipment, load convention and mapping version identifies a comparison series. A group key within an exercise distinguishes technique, top, backoff or accessory work. Ordinals address sets within that group. A running role key identifies easy, long, run/walk, controlled or faster work, with a versioned structure/protocol. |

An effective calendar maps each occurrence to its originating **prescription revision** as well as its effective approved head. Carrying a session forward copies the link. Adding a third week does not create a new prescription identity for the first two. A change of priority, protection, hard floor, baseline, allowed operator, joint profile or block boundary needs an explicit block proposal with an effective date. Its preview identifies affected future weeks. Activate a block revision with compatible retained weeks, or atomically approve explicitly displayed replacement prescriptions with it. Never leave active future detail in conflict with the newly approved block. A block-only receipt confers no authority for a later week.

## Canonical prescriptions

Use immutable discriminated types and ordered sequences. Keep integers for repetitions and seconds, decimals for quantities, canonical units, exact finite endpoints and stable keys on serialized values. A fixed value has equal low, nominal and high. For optional alternatives, encode mutually exclusive named choices or validate the full Cartesian product of permitted endpoints. Validate minima against floors, maxima against ceilings, and the fast end of a pace range against any speed limit. Reject unrepresentable equipment settings. Display rounding never alters a sealed value.

**Strength.** A session carries its logical key, purpose, ordered exercises, session notes and reserved time. Each exercise includes movement, variation, label, equipment, load convention (e.g. total external kg, per-hand kg or bodyweight), notes and ordered groups. Each group carries stable key, kind (warm-up/working), programming role, ordered ordinals, exact rep/load targets per ordinal, and fixed or variable rep scheme. Preserve a nominal vector such as `[7,6,6]` even when every ordinal permits 6–8. Attach a typed effort target (scale and version), distinct hard execution cap, and rest in seconds between sets/groups with an explicit last-set convention. A bodyweight task has its own convention. Never equate a missing load with zero kg. Approved flexibility specifies allowed endpoints or alternatives, applicable ordinals and stop/shorten cues. A cap exceedance remains visible in the actual report even if the athlete completed the set.

**Running.** A session carries logical key, role, purpose, primary quantity and time convention. Represent continuous easy/long work as one work step, optional warm-up/cool-down as explicit ordered steps, and run/walk or controlled/faster work as ordered steps or bounded repeats. Each step has a stable within-session key, locomotion, purpose, primary duration in seconds or distance in metres, exact targets, optional secondary bound, effort and cap, notes and approved flexibility. A repeat specifies count, work, *between*-repetition recovery and optional explicit final recovery. The expanded order is authoritative. Work, recovery, elapsed session time and actual running versus walking have separate totals.

Effort uses a named scale (e.g. anchored running RPE 0–10 with speech cue). Pace is seconds/km, smaller seconds being faster. HR can be absolute bpm or a particular percentage method such as HR reserve with its dated resting and maximum anchors. Carry reference ID/version, protocol, units, conditions, measurement dates, and which cue governs a conflict. An HRmax percentage and HR-reserve percentage require different references. Missing or stale references disable the dependent cue. Use familiar duration and effort when eligible. A controlled or faster purpose describes the task without assigning a physiological threshold. Distance-primary work is admissible only if calendar/time and all other hard bounds can be guaranteed at permitted endpoints, e.g. a bounded route and explicit time stop with an approved shortfall option. Otherwise present a conflict or a separately feasible time-primary choice.

For four 60-second work repetitions with 60 seconds between them, 600 seconds warm-up and 480 cool-down, the full prescription is 1,500 seconds. Adding a fifth rep costs 120 seconds, because it adds one recovery. Explicit final recovery adds a further 60. The v1 repeat continues to expand every listed step on every repetition. Any adapter must account for the exact canonical expansion.

## Observation and confirmation at decision time

E4 consumes a frozen, owner-scoped set of normalized facts. For each fact require source/account and entity identity, observation revision or canonical content digest, mapping/comparison version, typed fields and units, reported provenance, support IDs and status, occurrence time and precision, source/report dates and zone, retrieval/recording and earliest known availability. Include the query window, collection cutoff, coverage, unresolved issues, denominators, exclusions and contradictory facts. Keep a distinction between an explicit zero, unknown, partial coverage, an unsupported value and an inapplicable measure. Totals from distinct subsets may be lower bounds. Date/title similarity cannot establish identity. An aggregate without identifiable support cannot establish execution of a particular set.

D1 owns read consistency, calendar alignment, source/account scoping, units, deduplication, comparison predicates and coverage definitions for the views it provides. E4 owns the immutable decision snapshot, task/slot mappings, confirmation, action eligibility and current-support check. Coordinate identifiers and correction/tombstone semantics, occurrence versus availability, comparable conditions, representative-week exclusions, aligned interval facts, and a transaction-consistent way to compare current support. Do not call D1B’s independent report transaction as an approval lock. Recheck relevant support in the command transaction, locking canonical rows and source references in stable order or validating a writer-serialized scope revision covering inserts, deletions and issue changes. If a source cannot provide that guarantee, keep the affected operator unavailable. Latest-state history alone cannot reconstruct what an earlier decision knew.

| Use | Eligibility and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Descriptive history | Separately display confirmed and inferred working exposure, origin, coverage and omissions. An ended Hevy workout with positive reps may be descriptively useful. Conflicting units or uncertain membership preclude a combined total. |
| Initialization | Filter by available-at cutoff, current regime, movement/role, equipment and convention, completeness and relevant conditions before selecting recent representative exposures. Prefer 3–4 representative weeks in the last 28 days and 2–3 comparable task exposures. A stable low-frequency task may use up to 56 days with a reason. Preserve spread, dates and exclusions. One credible exposure may justify a familiar repeat. Ask for a narrow confirmation or defer an unsupported number. |
| Positive progression | Requires the operator-specific evidence below, an approved predecessor, relevant tolerance, an authorized rule and enough time. An approved prescription, historical peak, missing RPE or partial slot cannot establish successful completion of the required task. A partial can contribute observed work. |
| Comparable outcome | Pair actual baseline and follow-up on the same task, range of motion or running protocol, units, conditions and relevant effort. Show comparison window, count and counterevidence. Report direction with uncertainty or mark unknown if incomparable. Select eligible tasks before looking at the direction of change. |

**Batch confirmation.** Display the exact dated list for two or three relevant workouts, mapped exercise, equipment convention and ordered candidate sets with load and reps. One action can confirm completion and the displayed values for selected members. The athlete can select completion only, exclude specific ordinals, correct a value separately, or leave an item uncertain. Preview the field mask and selected/excluded/uncertain members before submission. A broad report of usual frequency can support initialization at that scope. A workout-level yes with no enumerated sets has no ordinal coverage.

Bind the trusted actor and owner, command ID, presented payload digest, per-member source/revision and child identity, field masks, exact displayed values, response, mapping version, confirmation time and any superseded assertion. Reject a changed display or wrong owner on submission. Preserve the original inferred provenance and absence of a provider completion timestamp. Only the overlap of the current, matching content and affirmed fields gains athlete support. A correction appends a superseding answer, including withdrawal, with a new recording instant. Changed or deleted children, source support or mapping invalidate affected members for new decisions. Unchanged independent members remain usable. An athlete can report a new independently sourced actual if import support disappears. Keep the original decision and evidence available as of their recording times. Confirmation of history requires no invented slot. To claim a planned execution, explicitly map it to the logical occurrence and prescription in force.

**Running actuals.** Record summary verification, elapsed versus moving time, distance and attributed role separately from instructions. Optional athlete answers can establish completed work rep count and durations, recoveries, effort, manageability and subsequent relevant tolerance. Store whether duration was reported per rep or uniformly confirmed across identified reps. A verified 40-minute outing with instructions for three six-minute efforts establishes the outing only. Scoped confirmation of those three efforts can qualify for a future work-duration decision. A pace decision additionally needs aligned achieved time/distance or measured pace under comparable conditions. Whole-run averages, planned pace and aggregate split labels cannot supply it. These gates support ordinary progression without FIT or per-second telemetry.

## Review policy and the four reference values

Use the J3I-HYBRID-0.1 defaults below as **one candidate practical profile**. Present the athlete with a block’s objectives, familiar workload, hard commitments, allowed kinds of change and meaningful tradeoffs. Keep the parameter detail inspectable as a pinned configuration. Integrity of identity, units, temporal ordering, exact endpoint checks, safety restrictions, authority, approval and replay are engineering invariants. Step sizes, role gates, phase timing, search grid and policy choices have their own versioned values or conventions. An athlete preference can tighten an eligible option. Expanding the profile requires review.

| Reference | Set when | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial approved baseline `I` | First block approval pins familiar nominal vector, roles, units and evidence. | Cumulative movement/group, aggregate working-set and run ceilings. A week, calendar move or role rename cannot reset it. |
| Approved predecessor `P` | Open a review on the approved version in force for the actual work being reviewed, recording source week, prescription, target week and vector. For a repeated proposal keep the original review anchor, even if the calendar head advanced. | Denominator for all scheduled **and** discretionary positive changes within that review. Current head is a separate concurrency check. |
| Eligible actual reference `H` | Filter field-level current facts by attribution, cutoff, comparability, recency and support. Preserve multiple observations, missing fields and difficulty. | Determines which tasks can credibly repeat or progress. An incomplete week can narrow choices. It never increases headroom. |
| Temporary re-entry ceiling `R` | On material interruption or evidence that the prior envelope is currently unsupported, record affected roles, reason, supporting facts, numeric bounds when credible, next review and exit criteria. | Additional upper bound for the affected work. With no credible numerical reference, defer the number. A scheduled review date triggers reassessment only. |

**Review accounting.** Give each review an immutable anchor identifying block lineage, source prescription and completed review period, distinct eligible actual IDs, their content and cutoff. Reusing those actuals for another preview, new command, later week number, decline, move, correction or approval spends from the same per-review allowance. Derive the remaining room by comparing the entire resulting vector to the opening `P`, across all amendments and simultaneously permitted endpoints. An approved reduction also binds subsequent proposals to its lower effective prescription until reviewed. A fresh budget for a role requires a new relevant eligible performance after the applicable approval, review of that exposure and a new pinned predecessor. Close the reviewed period once per role and prescription under the owner/cycle lock. Several repetitions, ordinals or reports within that period cannot open several budgets. A duplicate import, late confirmation of the same performance, a second success in a different role or a correction alone cannot refresh it. Re-evaluate adverse facts immediately. Opening the next calendar week without new actuals may yield a familiar hold only where independently supported.

For a positive upper-body load, the largest permitted endpoint is bounded by the minimum of `P.nominal × 1.025`, `I.nominal × 1.05`, `R` when active, and other block or equipment limits. Check candidate low, nominal and high, including new flexibility if nominal stays fixed. Preserve a previously permitted range on a hold only if it still satisfies the active contract. For running, compute each dose dimension and the weekly sum in integer seconds. Keep approved duration and its execution range separate from reserved calendar time.

**Interruption and restoration.**

| Event at review | Effect and authority |
| --- | --- |
| One explicitly missed outing (e.g. venue closed) | Record a miss and any unaffected work. No dose debt and no automatic reduction of `I` or `P`. A safe, still-supported familiar hold can be proposed. Missing reports stay unknown. |
| Reduced performance or partial actual | Record `H` at its observed level with context. Withhold the affected rise. If the old dose is no longer credible, propose a conservative re-entry ceiling at supported familiar work. A concerning restriction can require an immediate stop/defer. |
| Material interruption, travel or material loss of tolerance | Open re-entry review on the affected role before generating further numeric work. Carry the tighter supported ceiling across dates, moves and proposals. A new difficulty, loss of support or restriction tightens the ceiling or defers the task immediately. An availability-only interruption can affect placement without implying a measured loss of capacity. |
| Approved prescription reduction | Preserve original `I` and review anchor. The reduced prescription binds immediate amendments. At the next fresh review it is the predecessor for the performed reduced work. No reversion to the pre-reduction value when travel ends. |
| Restoration | Initial profile requires resolved constraints, two distinct comparable manageable exposures at the re-entry prescription, a representative completed week, relevant feedback including subsequent affected work, and a fresh review. Propose one ordinary bounded step from the reduced predecessor. Explicitly identify the new ceiling or retirement of `R`, exact evidence and remaining `I`/step caps. Require reviewed restoration authority and athlete approval before activation. If facts are missing, retain/tighten or defer. Faster restoration is a separate, disabled operator. |
| Genuine new context | Changed goal, movement convention, equipment or training regime may warrant an explicit successor block or material baseline revision. Show differences, continuity or loss of comparability, credible new actuals, reason the previous baseline no longer applies and all resulting caps. Require new block approval. A wish to exceed a ceiling cannot establish a new context. |

**Practical progression choices.** Start with a familiar six-week block where appropriate (ordinary profile range four to eight), review weekly, at midpoint and end, or sooner on a restriction or material correction. Week validity is set by the individual block. Phase changes describe intent. They never schedule unearned increases, tests, deloads or tapers.

| Operator | Candidate default and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Reps and load | For familiar variable-rep work add **one total rep per developing movement in one group**, deterministically on the first eligible ordinal within range. Fixed-rep groups keep their reps. Load uses the smallest positive available step within +2.5% upper/+5% lower per review and 105%/110% of initial nominal respectively. For variable reps require all working ordinals at the top before load/reset to the explicit lower rep prescription. One fully confirmed, within-target manageable ordinary effort-aware success may suffice. Require two comparable nominal successes with credible manageability if set RPE is missing, or for near-limit work. A measured cap breach or unresolved shared difficulty blocks related progression. Hold a successful maintenance or technique task when no increase serves its goal. |
| Alternative for accessories | +1 on every set of a two- or three-set moderate/accessory group is easier to execute uniformly but increases total work two or three times as much. Keep this named alternative disabled initially, and heavy/fixed technique work outside it. To enable it require independent role-specific review, demonstrated tolerance, an explicit block option and no simultaneous set/load increase. Track rejected useful changes in E4.5. |
| Weekly working sets | Add at most one set to an eligible developing movement per review, within floor(25% × its predecessor weekly count). Both the movement and whole-week counts must remain within their respective initial weekly counts +2. Secondary counts normally hold. This keeps trials interpretable but can prevent useful growth in multiple developing movements. Keep both bounds initially, including the zero allowance at 1–3 sets. No accumulating +2 each week. A larger aggregate or low-count exception requires a named reviewed profile. Require representative counts, at least three eligible exposures over roughly two weeks, tolerance, opportunity and a reason that current progress warrants a trial. After a trial require at least two comparable exposures and a completed representative week before another. No positive set and rep/load change on the same movement. |
| Easy or long duration | Increase one familiar outing by up to 300 seconds. Total weekly increase ≤ min(600 s, floor(0.10 × predecessor weekly seconds)), cumulative ≤ initial weekly seconds +1,200, long role ≤ initial long seconds +600. Require two comparable completed manageable outings at the current role and representative weekly exposure. Long extension also requires credible recent long-task and subsequent lower-body tolerance. If the exact limit is 264 seconds, a five-minute increase fails. |
| Additional running axes | Keep familiar structure initially. Later candidates from J3I §4 include frequency +1 with weekly time redistributed and two representative weeks, established quality +≤120 work seconds per review and +≤240 cumulatively with two confirmed actual structures and tolerance. Preserve recovery and validate resulting total. Run/walk can replace ≤120 walking seconds and ≤10 percentage points per review at fixed elapsed, ≤240 seconds and ≤20 points cumulatively. Preserve the approved minimum walk. First quality entry (Q0) needs its own zero-to-entry envelope, eligible easy history and two total manageable Q0 exposures before transition review. No automatic pace, intensity or density increase. |
| Local hold, reduction or defer | Validate even a familiar hold against current safety, protection and feasibility. A time-only omitted accessory may leave a supported independent group eligible. A top-set success with a difficult backoff requires a shared-difficulty check. Offer an authorized local reduction, e.g. one fewer affected rep, or explicit block revision if a floor would break. Session RPE, HRV and general readiness cannot certify individual set compliance. |

The larger accessory step and looser aggregate budget are reviewable alternatives. The ordinary effort-aware gate avoids imposing repeated success on every familiar movement. Repetition of comparable evidence remains important for uncertainty, near-limit efforts, set trials, restoration and coupled changes. The full predecessor-parameter crosswalk is retained in [J3I §4](research/curation/j3i-001-integrated-hybrid-methodology.md#4-consolidated-numerical-configuration).

## Constructing and reviewing a feasible week

1. Load the approved context, block revision, review anchor, actuals known at cutoff and applicable authority. For initial construction use a proposed block and its credible familiar baseline. Reserve fixed events and retain observed/started days. On a new restriction recommend stop/defer for affected work immediately. Return a conflict if a hard commitment cannot currently be honored.
2. Enumerate a finite domain from the block’s role templates and authorized operators. Include a supported familiar hold. Validate independent local changes against actuals, exact equipment increments and all bounds. Classify **every** positive dimension, including a widened endpoint, added set, changed run fraction, faster pace, denser recovery or extra outing. A reduction elsewhere cannot erase that classification.
3. Compose eligible changes under the joint profile, then revalidate exposure floors, secondary outcomes, weekly totals, time and spacing. Under **J-DEFAULT**, an eligible upper-body rep/load change may accompany a separately eligible run change. Permit at most one positive lower-body group or lower-body set trial per review. Choose between a positive lower-body change and positive running demand. Reject frequency redistribution plus lower-body progression even at equal total time. An ambiguous movement’s lower-body burden needs clarification or the conservative classification.
4. **J-EASY-SMALL** is the only initial proposed exception. It permits one familiar lower group +1 total rep **or** the smallest available load step ≤2.5%, together with one ordinary easy outing +≤300 seconds. Require two manageable comparable exposures per role, representative joint history, no unresolved secondary or generalized difficulty, all independent gates, exact time and dose bounds. Exclude set increases, raised effort caps, long/quality/frequency/run-fraction increases and other positive lower/running axes. Requires independent review, operational authorization of this combination, an explicitly amended block naming it and approval of the precise week.
5. Place sessions into eligible local windows, checking equipment and exclusive resources, per-day totals, weekly budget, fixed start/date versus movable purpose and occurrence-specific exceptions. Reserve worst-case work, warm-up, all applicable rests, recovery, cool-down, setup, transitions and route/prep time exactly once. For strength, pin an execution-time allowance per set and exercise setup plus all rest allowances. For running, sum expanded step maxima plus any separate preparation. A five-minute longer reservation alone permits no additional work.
6. Check intersecting sessions and spacing on both sides of the detailed week, including prior actual times where known, adjacent approved weeks, provisional boundary reservations and fixed events across blocks. Start with at least the neighboring two local days and extend to every dependency of a longer spacing rule. Record endpoints (start/start or end/start), local zone, UTC instants and required elapsed seconds. Reject nonexistent local times. Ambiguous times need an explicit offset/fold. A future unplaced protected task needs either a compatible boundary reservation that can be honored on later detailing, or a reported unresolved constraint. Later proposals must revalidate in both directions.
7. Enumerate dates, finite starts (a five-minute local grid **plus exact fixed starts** in the initial design), ordered dose alternatives and permitted moves in stable order. Pin enumerator/ordering version, inputs, domain, deterministic candidate limit and count, sound prunes and reasons. Prioritize hard commitments, then feasible alternatives consistent with ranked objectives, preferences, minimal disruption and simple execution. Use a stable key only to break materially equivalent ties. Present meaningful tradeoffs between tied goals to the athlete. No solver or cross-discipline workload score is required.

Return a **feasible candidate** with the exact calendar and checks, even if alternatives remain unexplored. Claim optimality only with a defined objective and proof (unnecessary here). Return **infeasible within the stated domain** only after exhaustion or a named hard contradiction with proof, e.g. two required gym sessions and zero equipped windows. Return **search incomplete** if the limit was reached before finding a feasible result. Include examined/available options, limiting constraints with requested/available quantities and any authorized next search or block-change alternatives. Never turn a grid result into a claim about all possible starts. Only a feasible result can become a prescription proposal.

**Outcome review.** Display elapsed/upcoming session counts separately. Known coverage = (completed + partial + explicitly missed) / elapsed. Completion = completed / known. A zero denominator yields unknown. Report actual sets, reps and run/work seconds separately, including attributable partials and distinct unplanned activity. Link an imported outing and its manual report once. Next report comparable task performance (protocol, actual value, effort and conditions), then outcome direction for each ranked or protected objective. For example, two completed gym sessions meet a frequency commitment while strength direction remains unknown without comparable performance. One concerning observation prompts review, repeated comparable deterioration prompts a protection decision, and material safety conflicts warrant action immediately. These facts cannot establish causal interference, individual fatigue state or optimal stimulus.

## Rule and evidence authorization

Five decisions have different authorities. Synthetic research-preview code can enumerate, display, simulate approval and replay invented cases under a fixture mode that cannot activate an operational cycle. Independent scientific reviewers decide which empirical premises are supported for an exact scope. Independent practical reviewers decide whether a specified action, fallback and parameter profile are appropriate. An operational release authority then enables an exact reviewed rule/configuration for specified populations and actions. The athlete approves a particular dated block or week in that scope. Keep all five identities and decisions distinguishable in a preview. Today's archives supply candidates only.

**What J1 already allows.** `MethodRevision.claims` is a tuple with no minimum length. The manifest checks that each referenced claim exists and, for an approved method, is eligible. An independently approved method with an explicitly empty tuple in a reviewed manifest can therefore be returned by `methods(question)`. Retrieval is question-level for methods. It does not bind an executable, parameters, population, action or deployment authority. `ineligibility(key)` and release withdrawals apply to claims. E3 checks that the saved method still appears with the same content and that its claims remain eligible. Preserve those checks and the original empirical retrieval result and gaps. [Manifest validation](../backend/src/fitness_intelligence/evidence/catalog.py) · [Retrieval](../backend/src/fitness_intelligence/evidence/retrieval.py) · [E3 rationale](../backend/src/fitness_intelligence/planning/rationale.py)

**Smallest extension.** Add one versioned, reviewed **rule-authorization manifest** alongside J1’s pinned release, with one entry per operator/profile/scope combination. Keep existing claim, method and release formats readable. Require the following before enabling any entry.

| Manifest field | Validation and use |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and contents | Schema, immutable entry key/revision and digest, rule implementation version/content digest, parameter profile ID/version/digest (including inheritance/overrides), operator or permitted combination, J1 method revision/content and exact release ID/SHA-256, evaluation cases. A parameter edit yields a new identity. Reject unknown schema, digest mismatch and fixture records in operational selection. |
| Basis and scope | Discriminant `practical_judgment_only` or `empirical_dependencies`. Both require an approved practical method, rationale, alternatives, limits and visible gaps. Judgment-only requires an explicit rationale for independence and an empty required-claims list. The empirical branch requires nonempty exact claim revisions, scoped questions, approved source/transfer and result locators, with every method dependency included. Any scientific assertion displayed as support passes J1 eligibility and scope independently. Record allowed population/experience, current context, action, role, numerical bounds, contraindications, fallback and scope exclusions. Missing scope cannot silently match. |
| Attestations | Designated independent human scientific reviewer of each relied-on empirical source, claim and transfer plus reviewed J1 release. Record identity, role, date, reviewed artifact/revision, scope, rights/publication checks and decision. Independent qualified practical reviewer attests exact method, configuration, scope, action gates and stated gaps. Release authority signs off the exact mapping, conformance results, effective date and fallback. Store attributable decisions in version control with digests. Athlete and automated draft author cannot supply these reviews. |
| Activation and withdrawal | States proposed, reviewed, authorized, suspended/withdrawn or superseded, with effective instant, reason and successor if any. Only a current authorized mapping in an active pinned release qualifies. Carry forward withdrawals by exact identity. Before approval reproduce the pinned decision, then recheck current method content, required claims, transfer/scope, profile, rule and authority. A release switch must serialize with decisions (initially drain/restart the single-policy local process as in E3). Reject stale proposals. Historical views retain original content and annotate current ineligibility. |

**Proposed operator mapping for practical review.** A reviewer must identify every premise actually used. If it cannot be supported independently, disable that action until its exact empirical dependencies are eligible. The J3I §10.3 crosswalk identifies pending revisions, including proposed replacements. Archive citation alone supplies no eligible dependency.

| Action family | Candidate basis and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Place/move familiar work, repeat confirmed dose, preserve commitments, clarify a missing fact, reduce/defer for an explicit constraint | Scheduling within the athlete’s accepted constraints or repeating an observed manageable task can be justified as practical judgment. State the gap about optimal dose or recovery. Immediate stop/defer is available as a safety recommendation even without authority to prescribe a replacement. |
| Bounded reps/load, set trial, easy/long duration, frequency, quality duration/count, run/walk, Q0, optional pace adjustment | Each needs its own reviewed method/parameters/gates. A reversible increment from a demonstrably manageable comparable task can be submitted as a judgment-only trial with an explicit goal, uncertainty and review stop. A claim that a particular load strategy improves strength versus alternatives, a chosen dose preserves endurance, or an increment prevents injury requires the exact eligible claim and applicable population/comparator. Disable premise-dependent variants while unresolved. Faster pace also requires measured task evidence. |
| Joint change, protection review, re-entry and restoration | Conservative combination limits and a bounded restoration trial are reviewable allocation judgments with explicit scope and stop conditions. An assertion that elapsed spacing prevents interference, that a break causes a fixed loss, or that a restoration percentage is safe requires independently reviewed evidence for that assertion. No such assertion drives the candidate defaults. |

A withdrawn empirical dependency disables every entry using it. An omitted/withdrawn method, profile or combination also disables dependent use, including empty-claim methods. An independently authorized, currently supported familiar hold/reduction may be proposed with its own rationale. Otherwise ask or defer. Never relabel the failed rule as judgment-only. The existing J1 screening release and these proposed profiles grant zero operational E4 actions.

Use the existing trusted local owner binding and short transactions. Every new user-owned row carries a non-null user FK and composite owner/lineage constraints. ## Publishing handoff

A future adapter can read an immutable canonical export identified by schema version, owner, approved block/week prescription revision and digest, stable session key, scheduled local date/time, zone/offset where relevant, exact ordered exercises/steps, units, targets, flexibility and notes. A separate delivery request binds that identity to an explicitly selected owner-bound destination account/provider and the athlete’s destination authorization. Provider account selection and writes belong outside planning and local approval.

G1 determines which features can be represented, account eligibility, separate workout/calendar permissions and how to reconcile delivery. Surface unsupported units, intervals, rest placement or ranges as translation diagnostics before delivery. Persist remote identifiers, version mappings, attempt status and reconciliation keys only in a later adapter design when capabilities are known. A failed or partial delivery leaves the approved calendar usable through manual instructions or export. A changed remote routine is a delivery discrepancy. Importing a deliberate change into the plan would require a fresh local proposal and approval.

**Strength emphasis, familiar week.** An invented athlete ranks improving the overhead press first and preserving performance on a familiar continuous run second. **Running emphasis, week 5 of 6.** A runner protects two weekly strength opportunities including a comparable squat task.
