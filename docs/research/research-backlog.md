Packet 004 preserves consensus nutrition ranges in the research narrative. The draft excludes those ranges from randomized empirical claims. Investigate how a future catalog can distinguish empirical treatment effects, observational associations, measurement validity findings, consensus or professional recommendations, and candidate programming methods. Reviewed numerical guidance needs explicit units, conditions, population and limitations, with links to the underlying recommendation and its review. Direct endurance carbohydrate-ingestion evidence remains screening context pending extraction. Independent scientific review and a separate architecture decision precede any schema or release work.

Packet 005 adds three distinct measurement questions to that work. **Agreement** asks how closely a field estimate matches a reference. Limits of agreement describe the spread of individual differences, while a confidence interval around mean bias describes uncertainty in the average difference. Correlation alone cannot establish agreement. **Test-retest reliability** asks how much a repeated measure varies under comparable conditions. Typical measurement error quantifies noise. The change that matters to an athlete or decision needs a separate justification. **Longitudinal responsiveness** asks whether the measure tracks within-athlete change over time. Group-level validity leaves that question open. Preserve the reference protocol, units, time horizon and uncertainty for each result alongside the distinctions among controlled treatment effects, observational associations, consensus recommendations and programming methodology. For repeated measurements of an unchanged task with independent, zero-mean errors of equal variance σ², the difference has error variance 2σ² and a mean of n such observations has error variance σ²/n. Serial correlation, actual adaptation or changes in effort or protocol can invalidate these identities as models for training logs.

Consolidate overlapping candidate methods into a narrow policy set. For each decision, **source verification** establishes access, version, locator and study identity. **Claim appraisal** evaluates the measured contrast, uncertainty and limitations. **Population transfer** requires a rationale for applying that result to the intended athlete. **Candidate method review** assesses the action, alternatives and revision authority. **Numerical parameter review** examines provenance, units, bounds and validation. **Data readiness** identifies which observations support prescription, adherence and outcome evaluation. **Operational authorization** is a separate decision for a specific reviewed release and planner rule.
