## 1. Executive summary

J3-M02 revision 0.1 proposes a practical way to construct a coherent training block, not a new periodization engine. The block establishes why work is being performed, what outcomes matter, which commitments constrain the schedule, the initial familiar dose, and how far future weekly recommendations may change. J3-M01 remains responsible for local resistance-training prescriptions and progression. A future reviewed running methodology will fill the analogous running role. M02 does not create it by implication.

The recommended starting configuration is a six-week block, one detailed upcoming week, future week intents rather than detailed prescriptions, and a midpoint and final outcome review. Keep a productive, feasible habitual dose initially, including secondary work. Choose emphasis only for a meaningful priority, deadline or tradeoff. There is no required change of archetype or training increase merely because a new week or block begins.

Three independent layers make protection operational:

- **Outcome contract:** the athlete's target, reference, assessment and acceptable tradeoff. A target is not a constraint the software can guarantee to satisfy physiologically.
- **Exposure contract:** agreed training purpose, frequency/dose/specificity and calendar constraints. The validator can guarantee that a proposed schedule fits these conditions, not that actual execution or biological retention occurs.
- **Adaptation contract:** scoped floors, ceilings, progression rules, change budgets and review triggers, referenced to the approved block and current actual history.

The default running prescription is familiar, athlete-confirmed work only. Reserve time for future development; do not turn reserved minutes into an automatic mileage, speed, interval or HR-zone progression. Strength changes use the narrower of M01, block and equipment limits. No positive change requires a workload score, HRV, formal experiment or precisely estimated 1RM.

The three synthetic programs in §9 demonstrate incline bench emphasis with squat and long-run protection, 10-km emphasis with specified lift protection, and balanced development. They include missing outcome observations, local fatigue, a material availability interruption, explicit infeasibility and a productive unchanged continuation. The examples are candidate programming choices, not user prescriptions. Review the three M02 bundles as a whole before implementing any generalized planner.

### 2.3 Scope of action and architecture

M02 outputs a candidate block and its first week, or a named conflict/targeted question. It consumes athlete-confirmed objectives, current context, dated actual observations, rules and evidence eligibility. M01 consumes M02's approved movement roles, priorities, floors/ceilings, permitted changes, calendar and phase; it cannot silently change these inputs. Future running policy consumes the same contract. The existing planner/validator, immutable proposal, preview and trusted owner decision remain authoritative; M02a/b/c are review bundles, not parallel services, database schemas or J1 IDs. A general plan representation is a proposed extension, not an existing fact (see §10).

Out of scope: clinical rehabilitation/clearance, energy prescription, event-specific taper optimization, daily readiness scoring, latent fitness/fatigue models, causal learning, automated provider delivery, new interval/intensity/progression rules for running, automated evidence approval. Normal safety restrictions outrank all performance objectives; athlete may stop affected work immediately without waiting for plan approval.

## 3. Objective, archetype and reference methodology (M02a)

### 3.1 Candidate objective and protection representation

Use existing context revisions and block/weekly snapshots where possible. The following is a conceptual typed contract; field names are illustrative, not a proposed migration or a claim that the schema exists.

| Field | Semantics and validation |
|---|---|
| `objective_key`, `task_key`, `direction` | Stable outcome identity and exact task/measure; develop, retain, restore, assess, defer. Do not replace outcomes with generic discipline labels. |
| `priority_group`, `tie_resolution` | Ordered primary/secondary development and retention priorities; ties permitted. If scarce capacity forces choice between tied protected objectives, ask athlete rather than silently order by identifier. |
| `reference` | Actual dated task, value/unit, protocol, source/provenance, confirmation, comparable role/equipment/conditions and uncertainty. May be unknown; old PR distinct. Pin eligible observations and exclusions. |
| `assessment` | Existing or scheduled comparable opportunity; protocol, window, burden and substitution policy; fixed date only if explicitly protected. Avoid adding exhaustive tests to ordinary training. |
| `review_trigger`, `acceptable_tradeoff` | Athlete-agreed functional tolerance or qualitative tradeoff; consequence of missing evidence, possible adverse direction or failure to meet exposure. Not a measurement-error cutoff or performance guarantee. |
| `exposure_commitments[]` | Named task or group, purpose, unit, cadence, floor/ceiling, nominal amount, specificity, eligible substitutions and whether constraint is hard. Explicitly link to outcomes; one exposure can serve several objectives but counts once in each relevant aggregate. |
| `protected_date` / `protected_purpose` | Fixed local date/time/window when expressly agreed; purpose-protected movable slot if purpose, dose, neighboring constraints and feasibility are preserved. A move still needs review unless it is an already-authorized execution choice. |
| `restrictions`, `preferences` | Hard exclusions, equipment, time, event protection, explicitly approved spacing; soft day/convenience preferences and their order. No hidden relaxation. |
| `bounds`, `review_route` | Versioned scope, unit, reference, nominal and permissible endpoints, per-review and cumulative cap, exceptions and condition triggering weekly vs block vs objective review. |

A protection contract is not equivalent to a hard constraint on physiology. A long-run purpose can be a hard schedule commitment while an outcome of comfortable 75-min continuous running remains an empirical question. Exposure floor compliance is computed on the proposed prescription; actual deviations are recorded rather than forbidden by rewriting history. If actual training falls below the floor, the coach flags the deviation and reviews future feasibility, not the past prescription.

**Two-axis review:** for each protected outcome, report (a) planned commitment satisfied by candidate? actual adherence known/partial/missed/unknown, denominator and deviations; (b) outcome observed direction (favorable, stable within available resolution, possible deterioration, mixed, insufficient evidence) with protocol and alternative explanations. Neither implies the other. Example: all runs completed but no comparable long-run conditions -> outcome unknown. Example: a missed lift followed by a similar measured 1RM -> performance observed stable, adherence not complete; no causal conclusion.

| Outcome | Defensible reference/assessment | Exposure proxy and prohibited substitution |
|---|---|---|
| Maximal strength | Same lift/variation and protocol, measured max only if familiar and wanted; otherwise standard submaximal load-rep-effort task labeled performance proxy | Familiar specific loading; rep records or e1RM do not directly measure maximal strength. |
| Hypertrophy | Appropriate repeated size measure under similar conditions; if unavailable, unknown | Agreed muscle-relevant lifting; neither set counts, strength retention nor bodyweight prove muscle retention. |
| Aerobic capacity | Comparable laboratory VO2max when accessible; field observations separately labeled | Familiar aerobic work; wearable estimates not laboratory measurements. |
| Running-specific performance | Same timed distance/protocol and sufficiently comparable course, conditions and effort; race result distinguished from controlled test | Preserve run-specific exposure and appropriate session purpose; a cycling session or HR estimate not race performance. |
| Long-duration endurance | Agreed duration/distance and completion/effort/conditions; comparable prolonged task, not a shorter extrapolation | Familiar long-run opportunity and purpose; stable VO2max or weekly kilometres alone cannot establish long-run retention. |

### 3.2 Archetype selection

First identify a real decision: which work needs development, protection or reduced burden, and what competition or time constraints exist? Generate compatible archetypes and prefer continuity if an existing block is productive and matches current goals. Select the least disruptive feasible alternative that serves the athlete's ranking. Do not require all secondary work be reduced in emphasis. Do not label an athlete as a permanent archetype.

| Archetype | When suitable | Start and protect | Discretionary reconsideration | Change trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strength emphasis | Named lift/outcome priority, assessment horizon or limited development capacity | Maintain familiar running purpose/dose and specified secondary lifts; use relevant strength work from M01 | Nonessential lifting, optional run intensity additions or novel accessories if burden competes | New race priority, repeated protected run/lift problems, unavailable equipment or changed desired outcome |
| Running emphasis | Explicit run outcome or event, familiar relevant running and plausible time | Reserve run-specific work; preserve meaningful familiar loading for protected lifts, retain full existing lifting where feasible | Reduce unprotected lower-body accessories or move conflicting lift before dropping specific work | Strength preservation conflict, repeated run infeasibility, changed race/goal; no automatic severity from a single session |
| Balanced | Both development priorities valued, no meaningful need to specialize | Keep productive exposure across both, with distinct roles and selectively allocated progression | Redundant work/time-heavy accessories; optional extras | Explicit priority shift, persistent competing demands despite feasible local changes, or event deadline |
| Maintenance / transition | Interruption, temporary constraint, no credible development dose, or athlete preference | Current credible familiar work, retain specific agreed outcomes where feasible, conservative re-entry | Peak-load attempts, optional volume or intensity, testing burden | Evidence of current tolerance, context restoration and athlete-approved objectives justify return to development |

Balanced does not require equal minutes, sets, effort or adaptation; emphasis does not require an experimental concentrated conditioning protocol. If a hard secondary floor and primary desired exposure cannot coexist, show options with the cost to each objective, and seek explicit decision. No algorithm may infer the athlete's desired tradeoff from their most recent logged sport.

### 3.3 Current capacity and habitual exposure (Packet 009)

1. Freeze the decision `as_of`, owner, time zone, context/head IDs, observation IDs and versions, available-at timestamps, task definitions and provider coverage. Use occurrence time for training chronology and availability time for replay. Later imports/corrections can inform a new proposal, never an old frozen rationale.
2. Define eligibility before looking for improvement: same movement, load convention, equipment, variation/ROM, set role, actual ordered work and materially comparable effort/rest; running task, time/distance definition, course/conditions, purpose and effort when relevant. Separate confirmed, inferred, ambiguous and missing. Keep contradictory evidence and exclusions with reasons.
3. Choose recent representative 2–3 task exposures, usually within 28 days (M01 N01); extend with unchanged context for low frequency. Use the latest representative confirmed performance and inspect prior references. Old PRs, incompatible equipment and stale pre-break peak workload may support context or aspiration but not current numerical anchors. One comparable observation supports a tentative familiar starting point, not a sustained direction. Do not average incompatible tasks or select a best effort post hoc.
4. Summarize 3–4 recent representative completed weeks where coverage is credible: per-movement working sets and frequency, load/reps by role, session time and rest; run frequency, duration/distance, purpose, longest representative exposure, actual familiar demanding work, other sports and days available. Report ranges and denominators; do not make missing week a zero or pool sets, distance, tonnage and RPE into one number. Assigned versus completed versus tolerated versus associated with improvement remain distinct. Partial weeks, deloads and travel are explicit context, not silently removed.
5. If history is inferred Hevy data, retain its descriptive use, ask only for consequential confirmation (e.g. 'Were these actual working sets, on the same barbell?'). Do not fabricate confirmations or transfer to an M01 progression gate. If coverage sparse, seek a recent athlete-reported actual or conservative familiar assessment; if no defensible load/duration, offer qualitative options and defer precise prescription. After changed circumstances reassess task relevance before applying window rules.

Minimum for quantified starting week: objectives and priorities; current restrictions, equipment/availability; recent relevant actual exposure or credible confirmed athlete history; feasible load/time options; explicit protection and review. Optional: anchored set/session effort, local soreness, sleep/stress/fueling context, past productive schedules, course/weather, comparable task tests. No comprehensive daily questionnaire or high-resolution telemetry. Ask a question only if it can change an action or resolve a genuine conflict.

## 4. Initial training prescription and rolling-horizon structure (M02b)

### 4.1 From actual exposure to first week

Start from representative actual exposure, not the prior plan or the maximum historical week. Retain productive, tolerated work if feasible. In strength, classify each movement as priority, protected-specific, assistance, technique or discretionary. Choose familiar variation and equipment; map set groups and counts to task role; allocate sessions across current calendar. Delegate target loads, rep ranges, effort, rest and small adjustments to M01a/b/c. Use ordered groups and per-ordinal targets where required. Do not set volume based on lifetime PR or e1RM; M01 initialization and eligibility are the source of load decisions. Protect relevant squat, press, pull or other outcomes separately rather than treating all lifting as fungible.

In running, distinguish easy, familiar demanding and longer tasks, relevant actual frequencies, durations and distance. Copy a defensible familiar task at comparable conditions, not a new speed or intervals derived from VO2max/LTHR. If the athlete confirms a previously completed 40-min session including warmup, known repeats and cooldown, preserving that exact task is a familiar-work choice; it does not approve a progression rule or claim that repeats are optimal. Use the athlete's own anchored familiar conversational or effort description, with no new universal intensity zone. Record actual distance if observed; duration-based prescriptions do not impose a second distance target. A changed weather/terrain condition may require a separately reviewed familiar effort alternative. Running emphasis can reserve extra time and specify development intent, but unreviewed numerical increases remain conditional until J3B.3 or an independently approved specific prescription. Do not falsely promise a faster 10 km.

If initial exposure cannot fit, remove optional work in the athlete-agreed order, move flexible sessions, then consider a modest local reduction of nonessential work. Recheck all protection floors and task specificity. If unavailable historical task or hard bounds make preservation impossible, explicitly revise the block rather than generating a superficially compliant replacement. Preserve credible unrelated work where safe. A primary objective has priority over discretionary work but not a license to violate a secondary hard commitment.

### 4.2 Practical block horizon

Default 6 weeks (4–8 ordinary choices). The planning horizon creates opportunities to review; it is not a biological mesocycle constant. For a 4-week block, review weeks 1, 2 and 4 with fewer opportunities before claiming a trend. For 8 weeks, midpoint around week 4 and end review, same weekly adaptation. Short transition block may be appropriate for known travel or uncertain capacity; choose actual dates rather than set a universal interruption duration. An event may require a different horizon and protected date; defer event-specific taper prescriptions. Review feasibility before committing to any block length.

| Week | Intent | What is actually prescribed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish familiar dose and credible baseline, or use recent qualified baseline | Detailed dated first-week proposal; M01 initial work and familiar running |
| 2 | Continue or adapt if eligible, preserve protections | Generate only after actuals/context review; no automatic escalation |
| 3 | Midpoint: compare primary and protected outcomes, opportunity, tolerability and adherence | Upcoming week's exact candidate; optional familiar assessment within approved bounds |
| 4–5 | Continue development, retention or local adjustment; improve specificity if warranted | Weekly proposals based on observed execution; no prewritten dose ramp |
| 6 | Comparable assessment opportunity and next-block decision | Detailed week when due; no compulsory max test, deload or taper; defer test if not credible |

Packet 010 LT-P01/02/07 hierarchy: long-term goals/events and acceptable tradeoffs are versioned context; multi-block trajectory is provisional intention (possible later emphasis, dependencies, review trigger); active block has approved dates, ranked objectives, week intents, exposure/protection and bounds; only generated and approved upcoming weeks carry execution authority; session ranges define execution choices. Do not write a successor calendar for a race months away. A provisional successor is not approved simply because the active block is approved. Extending end date changes a block even if the training content remains identical. End of block may yield continuation of the same archetype, explicit extension, new block, transition or deferred assessment—not forced rotation.

### 5.2 Exact interactions and endpoint validation

Use distinct denominators: initial block nominal `S0,L0` and approved predecessor nominal `Sp,Lp`, and actual history `H` for credibility. For a primary movement set rise, `Snew <= min(Sp+1, Sp+floor(.25*Sp), S0+2, approved movement ceiling)` and total sets <= initial total +2 (or tighter). M01 N13 and N15 still gate; a successful session is insufficient. With `Sp=3`, floor(.75)=0 and no addition; with `Sp=4`, at most 5; from 5 to 6 only after eligible trial review and if initial ceiling permits. No extra set merely because another exercise lost a set. Counts include top/backoff groups once, not warmups. A reduction can be larger for safety but needs a compatible future proposal or conflict; a missed set is an actual deviation, not a new baseline.

For each load group candidate `Lnew>Lp`, enforce discrete equipment, `Lnew-Lp <= min(Lp*c_M01, block_step_abs, Lp*c_block)` for applicable caps, `Lnew <= L0*cumulative_multiplier` and approved absolute ceiling. Check M01 credible H, role and effort/rep reset. Example: 80 kg press, initial upper cap 84 kg, current 82 kg, step cap 2.05 kg: 84 kg may fit with 1 kg equipment steps, 84.5 fails cumulative even if predecessor step would fit; 82.5 from 80 fails step cap with 2.5 kg plates. A lower-body 140 kg baseline has proposed cumulative 154 kg, but a protected fixed 140 kg group remains fixed. No rounding upward to satisfy convenience. Check both nominal and maximum allowed endpoints (e.g. if a load range could permit 85 kg it fails an 84 kg ceiling even at nominal 83). If a required bound is absent, defer that positive dimension. No new week, role rename or reallocation resets block baseline. A true new block may choose a new baseline from current credible actuals with explicit approval; no laundering increases.

Repetition rises follow M01 N04 and approved bands, no load and rep increase together; load increase can reset variable group to lower endpoint, fixed-rep role remains fixed. Role-specific assessment may explicitly allow a different test only after review, with known equipment/time and safety. A protected run or lift is not a universal reduction ban: new restriction can make work inappropriate, stop/defer it and surface the conflict instead of silently altering the plan. B12 treats lower-body load, reps, sets and run dose/purpose as separately inspectable changes. A planned run extension paired with lower-body set increase is disallowed under default even if total hours look unchanged. Upper-body change need not be blocked solely because an easy run is retained. Reserve time is not counted as exposure.

## 6. Calendar construction, feasibility and adaptation (M02b/M02c)

### 6.1 Deterministic calendar construction

Generate candidate placements for named sessions from actual local availability windows and equipment. Protect fixed event dates first; distinguish fixed date from movable protected purpose. Preserve already-started days and recorded execution. Allocate priority and protected tasks, then required specific secondary exposures, then discretionary work. Validate combinations rather than greedily locking one slot that makes a feasible alternative impossible. Enumerate finite alternatives using a deterministic ordered list of windows, approved substitutions, permitted dates and stable keys; an exhaustive solver or separate optimization infrastructure is unnecessary initially. For a small week, try existing schedule first, then minimal valid moves. If a bounded search cannot exhaust plausible candidates, report search limitation rather than prove infeasibility.

Time budget includes general warmup, movement-specific warmup/ramp, every working set, rests, transitions, cooldown, required travel/setup buffer where it consumes availability. For examples use explicitly bounded maximum rest and conservative set execution time, not average historical duration alone. Check all allowed endpoints (e.g., extra reps can lengthen set; slower allowed pace can lengthen distance-based run). Never shorten required rests just to fit. If duration cannot be bounded, propose a feasible time/effort alternative if independently defensible or return conflict. Check equipment and known discrete load values; do not invent access to machines or substitute same-name movements across equipment.

Consider actual recent demanding runs, hard lower-body lifts and other sport tasks in previous/next two days and around boundary. Prefer priority quality and local overlap reduction where feasible, but no inferred recovery constant from elapsed time. Check explicitly approved minimum spacing using actual UTC instants and correct local zone including DST; specify start-to-start or end-to-start. Validate prior approved and next reserved/approved boundary windows, not just Mon–Sun. Future neighbor without exact session may reserve a conservative admissible window; if none, conflict/clarify. A preferred rest day is soft unless agreed hard. Two sessions same day require actual nonoverlapping time windows, adequate transition and explicit feasibility; not assumed possible or forbidden by physiology.

No physiological utility score. If meaningful tradeoff remains between equally ranked objectives, return choices rather than stable-ID selection. Name each conflict with constraint key, requested and available values, affected future slots/outcomes and options (move, reduce optional work, change availability, or explicit block review). Never silently relax a floor or date. A search limit is not a physical impossibility.

### 6.3 Authority and interruption boundaries

Five distinct actions: read-only review (no mutation); execution choice within an approved range (actual record only); changed weekly prescription or schedule preserving block (sealed proposal/approval); explicit block revision (dates, intents, floors/ceilings, archetype/protected purpose or changed constraints; affected scheduled future weeks included or explicitly withdrawn); context/objective revision (athlete's goals, restrictions, availability, important dates and tradeoffs) followed by appropriate plan proposal. A context change alone is not approval of a replacement schedule. If current plan becomes unsafe or infeasible, flag conflict against current context, preserve history, advise stop/defer affected work; old approved head is not evidence of present safety. A restriction cannot be waived for performance. A voluntary missed-work deviation is not a retroactive revision.

Packet 010's calendar interruption, dose interruption and capacity/tolerance concern are distinct. For a single miss with credible capacity, continue remaining work or propose a small redistribution; no debt ledger. For several missed sessions or material retained-exposure change, inspect actuals and choose introductory exposure appropriate to current credible history; no universal break duration threshold. A single easy post-break lift does not prove full weekly tolerance. If return-to-training after illness/injury needs professional clearance, defer. If circumstances make old commitments impossible, propose explicit revision with tradeoffs, rather than claiming a substitute maintains the same outcome. Review actual subsequent tolerance before new positive changes. An extension or successor is always explicit, not an auto-renewal.

## 7. Block review, continuation and transition (M02c)

### 7.1 A practical review record

At each review freeze the context, current approved block and detailed week, current evidence/rule pins, observations available as of the decision, and the relevant historical references. Record: elapsed slot coverage and known adherence, intended versus actual exposure by task, deviations and reasons (athlete-reported versus unknown), primary/secondary outcome status and task comparability, current burden and practical fit, upcoming commitments/restrictions, and feasible alternatives. Keep the denominator of comparable observations, not just the best set or run. Report directional change and absolute raw units; if no test, leave the intended outcome unknown and report proxies separately. A stable submaximal performance task can justify continuing a prescription without a claim that maximal strength or hypertrophy is unchanged.

Use a short decision summary: `continue`, `local_review`, `weekly_proposal`, `clarify/defer`, `block_review`, or `context_review`. These labels are conceptual, not new application statuses. The review can offer several feasible alternatives; preference for continuity is conditional on goal fit, safety and feasibility. A proposed change documents what is preserved, exactly what changes, why, which evidence is descriptive or pending, which constraints and references were applied, and what to observe next. Do not maximize a score across incomparable objectives. When a primary goal advances and secondary outcome is uncertain, continuation can be reasonable if the exposure remains credible and the athlete accepts uncertainty; don't label preservation successful. If the athlete does not accept risk of uncertainty, seek an acceptable measurement opportunity or explicitly renegotiate rather than silently replacing the target.

### 7.2 Rolling-horizon transition examples

- No imminent event and favorable comparable outcomes: keep the archetype and familiar work. At horizon end, offer a new block of the same intent or explicit extension using current references; do not force a novel exercise selection or periodization change. An unchanged weekly target within an approved horizon is not a new block, but a newly detailed week still requires normal approval.
- Race planned months later: active block plus provisional running-emphasis successor, with a review trigger when event approaches or running performance warrants. No assumed taper or exact future session targets. A changed race date revises context and forces re-evaluation of dependent commitments before approval.
- Planned travel: record dated availability exception before generating that week. If current block allows a reduced transition week consistent with protections, propose it; otherwise show an explicit temporary block revision. Do not edit prior approved weeks or schedule past sessions. Post-travel work is conditional on actual retained exposure and present capacity, not fixed calendar restoration.
- Recurring scheduling difficulty: preserve meaningful outcomes and simplify sessions if feasible. Ask whether the desired goal remains current, rather than calling the athlete noncompliant or permanently changing preferences. If athlete values social cycling instead of running, capture revised objective explicitly; cycling may be a separate activity, not equivalent run kilometres or proof of running retention.

An assessment may be deferred due to weather, fatigue, changed equipment, preference or restrictions. A missing final assessment is neither success nor failure. If a continued block is otherwise justified, set a credible future opportunity or explicit outcome uncertainty; don't force a max test to satisfy a data model. A provisional transition can be re-planned without execution authority, but if it changes an approved current commitment, the approved block/context must be revised through the existing workflow. Store only decision-relevant intentions and versioned context, derive summaries from canonical history, retain earlier decisions and counterexamples. No automatic renewal, arbitrary training-debt accounting or inferred physiological decay.

## 8. Deterministic candidate decision procedure

### 8.1 Conceptual procedure (not application implementation)

The following pseudocode describes inputs/outputs and order. It must be implemented, if authorized, inside the existing planner/validator and review commands, not as a separate engine. `reviewed_rule` denotes a future authorized version; this document supplies no such authorization. Fixture behavior stays unchanged.

```text

        allocations = familiar_actual_starting_dose(habitual, archetype, current_tolerance)
        allocate named priority/protected-specific tasks before discretionary work
        protections = construct_outcome_and_exposure_contracts(objectives, references, allocations)
        if protections or time conflict: try reviewed local alternatives; otherwise record conflict
        intents = select_horizon_and_week_intents(objectives, events, availability, protections)
        bounds = scoped_limits(initial_nominals, protections, M01_profile, context, intents)
        # missing bounds never interpreted as unbounded progression
        strength = M01_initialize(allocations, references, bounds, equipment, rule_pin)

        # unknown running development is intent/reserved time, not executable new prescription
        candidates = enumerate_feasible_week_placements(strength, runs, availability,
                     protected_dates, purposes, adjacent_boundaries, permitted_substitutions)

            validate current restrictions, identity, equipment, finite target endpoints

            validate scope, task specificity, phase, events and protected neighbors
            validate evidence and methods used against eligible release and current policy
            if any fail: retain named reason, do not submit candidate
            else retain candidate + exact rationale facts and alternatives
    if none feasible: return named conflicts or search/observation limitation
    choose by objective priority, preservation, continuity, preference, minimal churn
    if materially different tradeoffs tied: ask athlete to select, do not auto-resolve
    return block candidate + detailed first-week proposal + future intents + optional provisional successor

submit_and_review(candidate, context_id, expected_head):
    # use existing sealed proposal workflow; no new authority
    freeze rule, evidence digest/retrieval, exact references, coverage and assumptions
    render exact before/after including block, weeks, slots, targets, ranges and conflicts
    explain empirical premise vs reviewed programming choice vs engineering safeguard
    if not independently reviewed/operationally authorized: research preview only, no activation
    trusted owner decides approve or decline
    on approval under existing owner/plan locks:
        recheck context/head, evidence eligibility/current withdrawals, current observation support,
            elapsed days, execution links, restrictions, full candidate feasibility and replay
        reject stale/changed inputs; require fresh preview, never auto-rebase
        persist decision and compare-and-swap head atomically; preserve all prior pins
    on decline: preserve head and record decision

    freeze observations, corrections, applicable approved versions and references
    attribute execution to in-force prescription at occurrence time; ambiguous remains unknown
    classify coverage, exposure adherence and comparable outcome directions independently
    if unsafe restriction: stop/defer affected work, flag stale/unsafe plan, context/block review
    if context/goal/event changes: capture explicit context revision and check dependent commitments
    if interruption: apply LT-P03/04 branches; no repayment and no automatic peak restoration
    if changed bounds, protected purpose, archetype, date horizon or objective required:
        return explicit block/context review with tradeoff alternatives
    if block horizon reached:
        return continuation/extension/transition/assessment alternatives, no auto-approval
    if insufficient observations materially constrain action: hold/clarify/defer
    strength_options = M01_review(approved, actuals, references, protected_commitments)
    run_options = hold familiar tasks; only approved running rule may generate new progression
    for combination in scoped options (including hold, reductions and permitted moves):
        validate all endpoints and joint changes against initial and predecessor references
        validate current availability, neighboring windows, commitments and safety
    if no feasible combination: return named conflicts and block review
    choose simplest feasible objective-aligned alternative, explain alternatives and uncertainty
    return read-only review / within-range execution / changed-week proposal as appropriate
    # future exact prescriptions go through submit_and_review; actual follow-up becomes new evidence
```

### 8.2 Deterministic selection and authority invariants

Hard validation precedes preference selection. Candidate reference and observation eligibility precede interpretation. Current and neighboring commitments are not soft penalties; they exclude violating candidates. For objective-compatible alternatives, prefer retaining current productive work, then the athlete's stated preference and relevant task quality, then fewer moves or changes; ties use stable ordering only when no material objective tradeoff exists. If no feasible candidate exists, preserve head and explain exact conflict (constraint ID, requested/available values, affected task and alternative). Do not claim exhaustive infeasibility if the enumeration was incomplete.

Stable task keys, plan-local movement identities and slot keys survive moves; role-specific set groups cannot be treated as interchangeable just because they share a movement. Freeze values rather than depend on mutable importer IDs alone. Historical decisions are replayed using exact rule and evidence release/digest and observation state available then. Current eligibility is checked before new advice approval; a withdrawn premise or stale context invalidates reliance, not the historical decision. A corrected observation can invalidate a candidate even if aggregates match. Replaying a decision never creates another approving receipt or reattaches the workout to a later head. No source, method or transfer gets an approval from model text. The current `synthetic.press.v1` and its exact eligibility gate are preserved.
